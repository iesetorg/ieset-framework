# Post-lock WIPO physical indicator alias: OWID conflict family

The eight case registrations, runner, sequential source locks, and the national geography adapter precede this repair. The first invocation through the geography adapter printed four fitted UNDP cases: life expectancy (1,351 complete rows, 193 countries), mean years schooling (1,240, 187), expected years schooling (1,284, 189), and GNI per capita (1,318, 189). Each received a `PARTIAL` verdict under the frozen rules. These numerical non-results were opened before this repair and are retained as part of the audit chronology. No run directory was written because the family runner writes only after all eight cases compute.

That invocation stopped at the fifth case before a WIPO coefficient or verdict was fitted. The WIPO Parquet `value` column had been loaded in the process, but the physical identifier guard rejected its `indicator_id` of `IP.PAT.RESD`: the pinned manifest and preregistration call the same series `patent_applications_resident`. The raw source hash and manifest hash still match all eight locks.

Before another measured invocation, a single key-only pass checked the physical identifier and selector columns for every remaining WIPO, WITS, and WDI file. The resident-patent file has 17,556 rows, with physical `indicator_id=IP.PAT.RESD` throughout and no physical `series_id` column. WITS export breadth has 5,308 rows and `indicator_id=NMBR-XPRT-HS6-PRDCT`, `series_id=unique_hs6_products`, `freq_id=A`, `product_code=999999`, and `partner_iso3=WLD`. WITS concentration has 5,129 rows and `indicator_id=HH-MKT-CNCNTRTN-NDX`, `series_id=product_concentration`, `freq_id=A`, `product_code=999999`, and `partner_iso3=999`; the registered outcome correctly labels it market concentration. WDI investment and population each have 17,556 rows and physical identifiers `NE.GDI.FTOT.ZS` and `SP.POP.TOTL`. Only the WIPO catalog alias differs from the physical indicator code. This pass did not examine outcome values or null masks.

This adapter retains the frozen manifest alias during source verification and packet construction. Only while the frozen patent numeric loader checks the already identified physical field does it supply `IP.PAT.RESD` as the expected indicator, then restore the registered alias. The national key filter, selected source bytes, registered temporal design, transformations, family-wise test, all sample and influence gates, model, and scoring restrictions remain unchanged. The original runner, prior adapter, and per-case source locks remain immutable. Replay uses this committed adapter and checks exact output bytes. This is an implementation repair after four numerical results were opened, so the chronology makes no fresh-holdout claim.
