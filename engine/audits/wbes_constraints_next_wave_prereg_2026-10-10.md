# Blind registration: Enterprise Surveys constraints and later aggregate growth

Family `wbes_constraints_next_wave_growth_2026_10_10` contains eight fixed descriptive country-survey-wave contrasts: four baseline business constraints, each paired with later real annual sales growth and later annual employment growth. A separate scout identified the source and initial code shortlist, then encountered isolated official outcome-number search snippets. The scout sent this registrant only exact source hashes, official codebook labels, methodology, and an indicator/key-only roster. This registrant did not inspect the selected source's `value`, `se`, or `N` columns, outcome null masks, coefficients, signs, or verdicts before fixing this design. This is not an independent external holdout and the family correction does not account for broader IESET search.

## Frozen estimator and gates

For each cell, fit unweighted OLS of later country aggregate growth on the baseline constraint (scaled per +10 percentage points), baseline growth of the **same** outcome, survey gap in years, country fixed effects, and later-wave period effects for 2010–13, 2014–17, 2018–21, and 2022–24. Use CR1 country-cluster standard errors and two-sided Student t with `G-1` degrees of freedom. The claim predicts a negative slope of at least 0.5 annual growth percentage points per +10 percentage points of the constraint. A same-size positive slope is a directional refutation. This is an ecological association; it does not track or identify firm-level effects.

The three selected rows per pair (baseline constraint, baseline growth, later growth) must have finite numeric values and finite `N >= 30` each. Constraint percentages must lie in `[0,100]`; growth percentages in `[-100,500]`. No alternate cut, country replacement, interpolation, imputation, winsorization, source vintage, or N threshold is permitted after results. Retain only countries with at least two **complete** intervals. A cell requires at least 70 complete pairs, 30 countries, three later-wave periods, and 20 countries with a baseline-constraint range of at least five percentage points across their complete intervals. Require full-rank design, finite positive clustered uncertainty, and squared residual-exposure concentration no greater than 25% in the top 1% of rows, 15% in any country, or 50% in any later-wave period. Failure of any gate is `INCONCLUSIVE` archive, with no qualified coefficient.

The eight-test local family alpha is 0.01, giving two-sided Bonferroni `p <= 0.00125` per cell. A gate-cleared expected-sign material effect is `SUPPORTED`; a gate-cleared opposite-sign material effect is `REFUTED`; every other gate-cleared estimate is `PARTIAL`. All results have zero causal, policy-effect, school, position, and featured credit. Later-wave aggregates can reflect prior shocks, changing firm samples and stratification, selection into formal activity, methodological changes, and survey timing. A confidence interval or p value cannot remove those limitations.
