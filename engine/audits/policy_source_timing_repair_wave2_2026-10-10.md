# UK policy source and treatment-timing repair, wave 2 — 10 October 2026

## Scope and rule

This bounded review started with 305 policy records whose exact date had no external reference in the event registry. Ten existing UK candidate records now cite primary legislation and, where needed, official implementation material. A statute's Royal Assent, a provision's commencement, an organisation's application date, and an observed outcome are distinct. No link, axis direction, canonical status, tested-policy flag, school score, or hypothesis verdict was promoted. Each reviewed record retains a treatment-onset blocker until its treated unit and provision-specific first exposure are coded.

## Registry disposition

The event registry still indexes 3,427 policies. Uncited full-date records fell **305 → 295**, and referenced full-date records rose **1,832 → 1,842**. These are source-presence classifications; the table above records the manual date/source check for this batch. Treatment-onset blockers rose **4 → 14** because the ten records now state the gap explicitly. `ready_for_event_study_timing` remains **1,828**; none of the ten joined it. Sourcing a legal event alone does not identify effects or make a policy tested.

The remaining 295 uncited exact-date records retain their flags. This batch does not assume their dates are correct. Follow-up requires primary-source verification and, where multiple provisions or jurisdictions are bundled, a narrower event/unit design before testing.
