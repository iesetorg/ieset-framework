# OECD sector productivity to next-year real compensation — frozen preregistration

This is **one correlated, three-cell prospective descriptive family**. Manufacturing (`ACTIVITY=C`), wholesale/retail trade (`G`), and information/communication (`J`) each have a positive directional claim: stronger real value-added-per-hour growth at `t` predicts stronger CPI-deflated labour-compensation-per-employee growth at `t+1`. The opposite qualified sign refutes the directional claim. No selected source value, null mask, observation-status mask, outcome sign, or model fit was opened before this registration. Only source hashes, fetch manifests, schemas, categorical series identities, and country/sector/year keys were inspected.

The frozen key roster is `data/pinned_evidence/oecd_pdb_sector_real_compensation_2026_key_roster.csv`, SHA-256 `d3e0e611f9f3407efbc646a5b2d00b3a1a3ccd685d15fd37136d215082cea18b`. It contains 2,179 *keys* at `t=1996..2023`: manufacturing 732 in 28 countries, wholesale/retail 715 in 27, and information/communication 732 in 28. A key is included only where OECD `GVAHRS` growth exists at `t`, OECD `LCEMP` growth exists at `t` and `t+1`, and WDI CPI keys exist at `t` and `t+1`. Key presence does **not** establish measured complete-case coverage.

OECD treatment is `MEASURE=GVAHRS`, `UNIT_MEASURE=XDC_H`, `PRICE_BASE=LR`, `TRANSFORMATION=GY`, annual, sector C/G/J, asset and conversion `_Z`: one-year real gross-value-added-per-hour growth in percentage points. OECD pay is `MEASURE=LCEMP`, `UNIT_MEASURE=XDC_PS`, `PRICE_BASE=V`, `TRANSFORMATION=GY`, same sector: nominal labour compensation per employee growth. WDI `FP.CPI.TOTL.ZG` is broad national consumer-price inflation. Only blank or `A` observation status and zero OECD GY unit multiplier are admitted. Eligible productivity growth is within ±50 percentage points; nominal compensation growth is within [−50,100] percent; CPI inflation is within [−20,50] percent. Real compensation log growth at each year `u` is `100 × [ln(1+LCEMP_GY_u/100) − ln(1+CPI_u/100)]`, bounded to ±50 log percentage points. No values are filled, interpolated, winsorized, or replaced.

The treatment-year roster is restricted to the fixed OECD core from the existing PDB promotion script. Countries with fewer than ten complete treatment years are excluded. Every sector requires at least 450 country-years, 22 countries, and 20 years represented by at least 12 countries. The fixed equal-row panel is `real_pay_growth_(t+1) ~ productivity_growth_t + real_pay_growth_t + country_FE + treatment_year_FE`, with CR1 country-cluster uncertainty and two-sided Student `t(G−1)` p-values. A design fails if the estimator is rank-deficient, residualized productivity SD is below 1.0 percentage point, residualized real-pay SD is below 0.5 log percentage point, one country supplies more than 20% of residualized productivity sum of squares, or the top 1% of rows supplies more than 30%. Five country hash folds use `int(SHA256(ISO3)[0:8],16)%5`; every leave-fold-out fit requires at least 320 rows and 17 countries.

Holm adjustment covers exactly the three registered sectors at family alpha 0.05. **SUPPORTED** requires beta ≥+0.05 real-pay log percentage points per one productivity percentage point, adjusted p≤0.05, and positive slopes in at least four folds. The same gates with beta ≤−0.05 and four negative folds yield **REFUTED**. An estimable result missing the signed conditions is **PARTIAL**; any sample, variation, concentration, or estimator failure is **INCONCLUSIVE**. The source locks must be individually committed after the registration commit and before any selected-value access; exact replay checks all three packets.

This family is distinct from aggregate productivity-compensation decoupling and the existing PDB productivity-to-employment test: its outcome is next-year sector real pay per employee, not employment. The three sector results are not independent replications. National CPI is an imperfect sector cost-of-living measure and pay per employee is not median hourly wages. Demand, bargaining, hours, worker mix, and simultaneous sector shocks remain confounders. Sectors and priors were chosen after related archive visibility; the within-family Holm cutoff does not correct archive-wide selection. This family grants zero causal, policy, school, position or featured credit. Raw and country-level values remain local under OECD attribution and World Bank CC BY 4.0 terms.

The fetch manifests identify the [OECD PDB dataflow](https://sdmx.oecd.org/public/rest/data/OECD.SDD.TPS,DSD_PDB@DF_PDB,2.0/all?format=csvfilewithlabels) and [WDI CPI indicator](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/FP.CPI.TOTL.ZG).
