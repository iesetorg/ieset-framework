# Blind source-key plan — protected land, forest cover, and food production per person

This is one new-ID two-outcome descriptive family derived from archived `protected_land_food_security_emissions_panel`. The archived generic runner used WGI government effectiveness and OWID CO2 intensity; it did not directly test protected-area expansion, net forest cover, or food production per person. These two endpoints are non-nested, but share one sample and one family. No old verdict or school claim is relabelled.

Pinned source schemas and hashes are specified in both hypothesis files. The WDI files use country ISO3, year, and value. FAOSTAT's normalized production file uses M49 area code, Item Code, Element Code, Year, Unit, and Value; the codebook identifies `Cereals; primary` Item 1717 and `Yield` Element 5412 in kg/ha. Source-key inspection, without values or null masks, found 172 ISO3 economies with all specified keys. The exact sorted roster and key digests are to be committed in a separate metadata-only lock after this registration passes full schema validation.

The 1995–2005 cereal-yield growth condition precedes 2005–2015 terrestrial protected-area-share change. The later 2015–2023 endpoints are annual net forest-cover-share change and annual log food-production-index growth less population growth. The shared complete-case rule and every threshold are fixed in the YAML. Forest uses a two-sided p<=0.025 rule. Food uses a one-sided p<=0.025 noninferiority test with a minus 0.5 annual log-point per 5-point exposure margin. This corresponds to about 4% cumulative eight-year difference and is a material-penalty bound, not proof of zero food loss. The two confirmation decisions use at most 0.05 family-wise alpha by Bonferroni.

The source lock must be committed before reading selected measurements or null masks. The runner must be committed before creating result artifacts. The run must respect all gates, disclose any post-observation correction, and exact-replay all artifacts. Any visible result is descriptive calibration only: zero causal, policy, school, position, and featured credit.
