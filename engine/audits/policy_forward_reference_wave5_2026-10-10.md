# Policy forward-reference audit: 36 starting links

**Scope:** source commit `5ad99f24810bca6190337246621ee13e6dd1e79c`, reviewed 10 October 2026. The starting movement corpus had 36 permitted candidate movement-to-missing-policy references. This bounded review adds **seven** candidate policy source records tied to official, dated, operative national instruments; removes **one** movement link because its referendum predates that movement; and retains **28** unresolved forward references. The seven records are **not** graduated hypotheses and do not confer policy performance, causal effect, movement-axis, or school-score credit. An unresolved row does not claim that no action exists; it means this review did not substantiate an exact action, date, and scope that supports this particular label.

The policy `enacted_date` is the documented adoption, assent, or publication event. `effective_date` is supplied only where commencement/application is documented; it is not assumed to equal enactment. Proposed packages, speeches, offices without a verified instrument, preexisting programmes, broad episodes, and government formation are kept out of the policy database.
