## Frozen endpoint, model, and verdict

After lock, require the three selected WITS counts to be finite, nonnegative integers. The raw `value` field is the selected numeric measurement; the Parquet also has no observation-status or method-break column, so no status-based assurance or exclusion is claimed. Drop only invalid selected triples, then reporters with fewer than six complete triples. Define `L(c,y)=log1p(count(c,y))`, forward outcome `Y=L(c,t+3)-L(c,t)`, baseline `B=L(c,t)`, and prior export-breadth change `M=L(c,t)-L(c,t-3)`. Regress `Y` on the WGI change `X`, `B`, `M`, log WDI real GDP per person at `t`, its `t-3`-to-`t` log change, reporter effects, and treatment-year effects. The sole primary coefficient is on `X`. Use OLS with reporter-cluster CR1 covariance, two-sided Student `t(G-1)` inference, and a 99% confidence interval. No adaptive covariate, transform, lag, country, or outlier selection is permitted.

The complete sample must have at least **1,200 pairs/100 reporters**, six complete years per retained reporter, at least **200 material improvements/75 reporters**, and **100 material deteriorations/50 reporters**. The primary design must be full rank and have finite positive cluster uncertainty. Residualize `X` on all fixed covariates and effects on that complete sample; the top 1% of rows may contribute no more than 30% of squared residual treatment variation, and a single reporter no more than 20%. Failure of any gate is **INCONCLUSIVE**. There is one fixed test with two-sided alpha 0.01 and a practical coefficient threshold of `0.10` log1p product-count point per one WGI point. **SUPPORTED** requires `beta >= +0.10` and `p <= 0.01`; **REFUTED** requires `beta <= -0.10` and `p <= 0.01`; all other estimable results are **PARTIAL**. Inconclusive, adverse, and null results must all be kept. The p cutoff addresses only this one test, not archive-wide selection.
