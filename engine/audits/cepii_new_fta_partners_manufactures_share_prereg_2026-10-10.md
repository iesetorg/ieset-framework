# New FTA-covered partners and manufactures export share: prospective descriptive registration

This one-test study fixes the CEPII exposure, WDI manufactures-export-share selector, country/year roster, estimator, and verdict gates **before selected WDI outcome values, null masks, or statuses are opened**. CEPII treatment values, WDI schema and country/year keys, source hashes/manifests, and prior hypothesis text informed registration. There is no claim of global archive blindness.

## Frozen design and treatment-only screen

The key roster is `data/pinned_evidence/cepii_new_fta_partners_manufactures_share_forward_2026_key_roster.csv`, SHA-256 `1f8d1b6c7a92ffcb3ffcdf226b3cf98fbf44f1bd8c5d6d3ee0f5027325ff82cf`. It contains exactly 5,028 unique country-year pairs in 212 current-country ISO3 codes, sorted by country and treatment year, t=1995–2018. Current-country selection uses the frozen ISO3 allowlist SHA-256 `cc306b7deb4ff39f16097111f5a48412bc49e268a7fa5dfc42a9c9427adf0e6b`. Each candidate has the CEPII treatment at exact t and WDI selector **keys** at exact t−1 and t+2. WDI key presence is an upper bound, not proof of a measured export share.

CEPII count must be a finite nonnegative integer. Freeze **event** if year-t positive net new partner count ≥1 and **control** if zero. Larger positive counts remain events; no magnitude cutoff search or weighting is allowed. The roster contains 1,013 event rows in 166 countries and all 24 treatment years, leaving 4,015 control rows. These counts use CEPII treatment values only; no WDI outcome values or missingness informed them. The event measures partner-count coverage expansion, not an isolated policy intervention; zero can include a net decline in covered partners.

After a **separate committed source lock**, read only selected WDI `value` fields; do not project `obs_status` during measurement or verification. Require unique country/indicator/year rows and finite WDI shares in [0,100] at both exact endpoints. Retain countries with at least five complete pairs. No interpolation, alternate WDI indicator, nearest-year join, outcome cap, or imputation.

Fit one prespecified OLS model:

`Share(c,t+2)−Share(c,t−1) = country_c + year_t + β_event·1[new FTA partners(c,t)≥1] + γ·Share(c,t−1) + error(c,t)`.

The zero-increase group is the reference. The baseline share is the exact t−1 value, before the exposure year. Because it is also subtracted in the dependent variable, mechanical coupling, measurement error, and regression to the mean remain interpretation risks. Country-cluster CR1 standard errors and two-sided Student t with G−1 degrees of freedom govern the event test. Require full design rank, positive residualized-event squared signal, and finite positive clustered uncertainty.

Complete-case floors are at least 1,000 rows, 70 countries, five pairs per retained country, 150 event rows in 50 countries and 15 treatment years, and 500 control rows in 70 countries. After complete-case filtering, residualize the event indicator on the baseline share, country effects, and treatment-year effects in the full model. Of its total squared residualized signal, the largest `ceil(0.01×N)` rows may contribute at most 15%, the largest country at most 5%, and the largest year at most 15%. These gates are checked **after** the WDI finite-value mask and cannot be claimed from the key roster alone.

If any custody, chronology, sample, event/control, rank, concentration, or uncertainty gate fails, return **INCONCLUSIVE**. Otherwise **SUPPORTED** requires β_event ≥ +0.5 percentage points and two-sided p ≤ 0.005; **REFUTED** requires β_event ≤ −0.5 percentage points at the same p; every other estimable result is **PARTIAL**. This is a one-test exploratory threshold, not a global multiplicity correction.

The WDI share can move because its merchandise-export denominator changes even when manufactures exports do not. Agreement selection, concurrent reforms, exchange rates, commodity cycles, source coverage and coding, and existing industrial trends can drive the association. The latest t+2 endpoint is 2020 and can be affected by the pandemic; no post hoc year exclusion is allowed. All verdicts are descriptive only and receive zero causal, policy, school, position, and featured credit.
