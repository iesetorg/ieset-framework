# Luxembourg national drift attribution — 2026-10-10

The national Luxembourg umbrella began in 2008, but its six unoverridden
axes were all scored from that year. Several rationales instead depended on
the 2020 free-fare and climate decisions or on 2024 EU-wide measures.
Membership in the EU does not make an EU decision a Luxembourg national
decision, and a movement start is not an enactment date for every later
policy.

## Source and coding review

- [Luxembourg's government](https://sip.gouvernement.lu/en/actualites.gouvernement2024%2Ben%2Bactualites%2Btoutes_actualites%2Barticles%2B2019%2B12-decembre%2B31-nouveauts-2020.html)
  dates state-funded national free transport to **1 March 2020**. The
  linked policy record supports weak spending and moderate sectoral-support
  coding from 2020.
- [Legilux's national climate law](https://legilux.public.lu/eli/etat/leg/loi/2020/12/15/a994/jo)
  is dated **15 December 2020**. The retained moderate environmental axis
  is attributed to 2020, without backdating the later carbon-tax outcome.
- The [European Commission's 2008 Dexia decision](https://ec.europa.eu/competition/state_aid/cases/227704/227704_944159_114_1.pdf)
  confirms a Luxembourg contribution to the joint rescue. It does not by
  itself establish the umbrella's strong *national financial-regulation*
  step or a recurring national spending-level magnitude. Dexia remains a
  contextual policy link; those two old 2008 codings are withheld pending
  a narrower review.
- [Directive (EU) 2024/2831, Article 29](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024L2831)
  requires national platform-work transposition by **2 December 2026**.
  It does not establish a Luxembourg labour-rule change in 2008 or 2024.
  The EU electricity-market reform likewise does not establish a national
  Luxembourg energy-security decision at the movement start. Both EU policy
  links and their unsupported national axes were removed from this movement;
  the EU records remain in the policy corpus.

The retained national axes each carry an explicit
`drift_attribution_year: 2020` and source basis. No measured policy effect
or new hypothesis verdict is inferred.

## Recomputed map

The generated national map remains **145 countries, 21 axes, 1789–2026**.
Comparing `data/derived/country_drift.json` before and after regeneration
shows exactly one changed country key, `LUX`. Luxembourg's composite is
now unscored through 2019, first scored in 2020, and is +5 from 2020 onward;
the former umbrella assigned +4 from 2008 onward. Nineteen Luxembourg
country-year composite entries changed. All other country series and the
map's date bounds are identical. Previously pinned movement-data vintages
and empirical result packets were left untouched; any rerun that elects this
new map needs a separately versioned source.
