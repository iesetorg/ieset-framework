## Source meaning and limits

The exposure is the [World Bank total-fertility-rate series](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.DYN.TFRT.IN), a national births-per-woman measure. It is not a policy intervention. The response measures cover different aggregates: [female labour-force participation](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SL.TLF.CACT.FE.ZS), [self-employment share](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SL.EMP.SELF.ZS), [services-employment share](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SL.SRV.EMPL.ZS), [GDP per employed person](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SL.GDP.PCAP.EM.KD), [households and NPISHs final consumption expenditure per capita](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/NE.CON.PRVT.PC.KD), and [gross fixed capital formation as a share of GDP](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/NE.GDI.FTOT.ZS). The frozen specification's shorter “private consumption” label for the fifth case must be read as **households and NPISHs final consumption**; that case is inconclusive and should not be promoted under the shorter label. The pinned source has no usable country-specific break flag, so method shifts remain a limit.

Fertility decline and the six outcomes can move together because of prior development, education, migration, social norms, economic demand, public policy, and measurement changes. Two disjoint ten-year response windows and country/anchor fixed effects do not identify any fertility, labour, or investment-policy effect. The four public calibrations have zero causal, policy, school-score, position, and featured credit. The two failures remain archived, with no fitted coefficients.

## Broader corpus ceiling

The existing IESET [large-family feasibility audit](large_family_feasibility_and_acquisition_2026-10-10.md) rejects multiplying products, ages, sexes, or correlated indicators into hundreds of nominal independent claims. This new programme adds four reviewable descriptive records, not a path to the remaining 500-record target. A larger causal or policy family needs new matched source bundles and designs rather than more cells from these same aggregates.
