## Fixed claim and measurement

A larger annual WDI renewable electricity output-share change from t-1 to t is predicted to precede a **higher** Eurostat medium-household electricity-price log change from second semester t to second semester t+1. The exposure is generation share in percentage points, not renewable capacity or a policy enactment. The outcome is `nrg_pc_204`, `freq=S`, `siec=E7000`, `nrg_cons=KWH2500-4999`, `unit=KWH`, `tax=I_TAX`, `currency=EUR`, and `period=YYYY-S2`. Eurostat's [official metadata](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/EN/nrg_pc_204_sims_ee.htm) describes household end-user prices and the consumption band. The [WDI metadata](https://databank.worldbank.org/metadataglossary/world-development-indicators/series/EG.ELC.RNEW.ZS) defines the renewable electricity output share and attributes its underlying series to IEA. These two measured series are observational, not policy treatment and outcome.

## Frozen model and decision

Regress forward log EUR/kWh S2 price change on the annual renewable-share percentage-point change, with country and treatment-year fixed effects, country-cluster CR1 covariance and two-sided t inference with G-1 degrees of freedom. Retain zero/small exposures; cap absolute annual change at 15 pp before price access. The full primary floor is 240 complete rows, 24 countries, 120 material rows in 20 countries, and at least seven complete years per country. The mandatory pre-2018 diagnostic uses t<=2017 and a floor of 150 rows, 24 countries, 75 material rows in 20 countries, and five years per country. In both models, top 1% residualized exposure variation must be <=0.30 and the largest country <=0.20. Rank, standard error and t inference must be valid.

This is a one-test local family at two-sided alpha 0.01, with a 99% confidence interval. The meaningful absolute beta threshold is 0.01 log price per 1 pp share change (roughly a 10% price change for a 10-point rise). Both primary and pre-2018 fits must have p<=0.01 and beta >=+0.01 for SUPPORTED, or beta <=-0.01 for REFUTED. An estimable primary that does not clear both directional gates is PARTIAL. A failed primary floor is INCONCLUSIVE. A directional primary whose mandatory diagnostic cannot be estimated is INCONCLUSIVE. No post-result window, family size, sample or threshold change is allowed.
