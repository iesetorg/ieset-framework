## Why the old records cannot be promoted

The current evidence audit contains 131 verified archive records with an estimator-floor failure whose treatment is `fraser_efw:summary_index` and whose outcome is WDI `NY.GDP.PCAP.KD`. Their claims range across labour, monetary, fiscal, growth and trade mechanisms; 128 have an estimator coefficient near +9.405×10^-17 and three have zero; all report R²=1 because the outcome and control used the same GDP-per-capita series. That result neither measures most stated treatments nor supplies a nonzero estimand. The old specs and run artifacts remain in the archive. These two new IDs test only direct measurable components; they do not inherit old verdicts, create reciprocal claim links, or award school/policy/causal/featured credit.
