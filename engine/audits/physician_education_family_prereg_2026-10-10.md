# Physician and education followthrough — frozen two-test preregistration

This is a closed family of **two** substantively distinct descriptive cross-country questions. The family-wide two-sided alpha is **0.01**; Bonferroni divides it by **2**, yielding **p≤0.005** for each. Both tests stay in the denominator even if one or both fail eligibility. The planned exposure window is **2000→2010** and later outcome window is **2011→2019**, avoiding the 2020 pandemic shock. No post-result endpoint switch, added proxy, covariate or country subset. Neither design identifies policy or causal effects.

The four recently frozen WHO WASH tests already crossed safe-water/sanitation gains with infant mortality/life expectancy outcomes and all were inconclusive. A basic-water/under-five-mortality variant was deliberately excluded here because it would re-test the same child-survival channel. Earlier OECD physician-density/amenable-mortality and broad education/demography archive work was visible before choosing this family. The local Bonferroni correction does not correct archive-wide selection.

## Frozen estimators, gates and verdicts

**Physicians → maternal mortality.** Select exact country-level WHO HWF_0001 physician density in 2000 and 2010 and WDI modelled maternal mortality ratio in 2011 and 2019. Physician values must be finite in [0,200] per 10,000; maternal ratios finite in (0,5000] per 100,000 live births with blank/A WDI status. Exposure is `(density_2010−density_2000)/10`; positive response is `ln(MMR_2011)−ln(MMR_2019)`. OLS controls 2000 physician density and ln(2011 maternal ratio). At least **50/91** complete countries, **15** with density gain ≥**2** per 10,000, and p90−p10 gain spread ≥**5** per 10,000. Full rank, finite positive HC3 uncertainty and positive residualized-exposure variation are required. After residualizing on both baselines, top country squared-exposure share ≤**0.20**, top five combined ≤**0.65**. Each of five leave-one-hash-fold-out fits must retain ≥**40** countries and a valid estimator. Meaningful absolute slope: **0.05 log point** per ten doctors per 10,000.

**Female education → fertility.** Select exact WDI female 25+ upper-secondary attainment in 2000 and 2010 and total fertility in 2011 and 2019. Attainment must be finite [0,100]; fertility finite (0,10], WDI status blank/A. Exposure is `attainment_2010−attainment_2000` percentage points; positive response is `fertility_2011−fertility_2019` births per woman. OLS controls 2000 attainment and 2011 fertility. At least **120/216** complete countries, **30** with attainment gain ≥**10** points, and p90−p10 gain spread ≥**15** points. Full rank, finite positive HC3 uncertainty and positive residualized-exposure variation are required. Top country squared-exposure share ≤**0.15**, top ten combined ≤**0.55**. Each leave-one-fold-out fit must retain ≥**100** countries and a valid estimator. Meaningful absolute slope: **0.01 births-per-woman reduction** per one attainment point.

All countries are equally weighted. Five hash folds are fixed by `int(SHA256(ISO3)[0:8],16) % 5`; they are sensitivity checks, not geographic strata. HC3 uses two-sided Student t with model residual degrees of freedom. A failed gate yields **INCONCLUSIVE** with no directional verdict. With all gates clear, **SUPPORTED** requires the positive meaningful slope, p≤0.005 and at least 4/5 positive holdout signs. **REFUTED** symmetrically requires the negative meaningful slope, p≤0.005 and at least 4/5 negative signs. Other estimable fits are **PARTIAL**. No failed case can be dropped from the family denominator.

WHO physician density may count active or registered doctors and does not measure obstetric staff or care quality. The WDI maternal mortality series is modelled partly using fertility, birth attendants and GDP. The female schooling share is cumulative across women 25+, while total fertility is a period synthetic rate. Economic development, health systems, norms, contraception and reporting can move both exposure/outcome pairs. Every verdict receives zero causal, policy, school, position and featured credit.

Official definitions: [WHO physician density](https://data.who.int/indicators/i/CCCEBB2/217795A), [WDI maternal mortality](https://databank.worldbank.org/metadataglossary/world-development-indicators/series/SH.STA.MMRT), [WDI female attainment](https://databank.worldbank.org/metadataglossary/sustainable-development-goals-(sdgs)/series/SE.SEC.CUAT.UP.FE.ZS), [WDI total fertility](https://databank.worldbank.org/metadataglossary/world-development-indicators/series/SP.DYN.TFRT.IN).
