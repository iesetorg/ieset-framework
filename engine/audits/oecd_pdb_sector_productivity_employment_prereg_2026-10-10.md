# OECD PDB sector productivity to next-year employment — frozen preregistration

This registers **one correlated, three-cell descriptive family** before selected OECD observations or their value/status missingness masks are opened. The cells cover manufacturing (`ACTIVITY=C`), wholesale and retail trade (`G`), and information and communication (`J`). Manufacturing and retail have frozen **negative** employment-growth claims; ICT has a frozen **positive** claim. A qualified opposite association refutes each directional claim. Neither sign is presumed causal.

The key-only source inspection found 749 admissible *keys* per sector, 1996–2023, in 28 countries after requiring keys for productivity growth at `t` and employment levels at `t-1`, `t`, and `t+1`: 2,247 country-sector-years total. These are not complete measured samples. The frozen key roster is `data/pinned_evidence/oecd_pdb_sector_productivity_employment_2026_key_roster.csv`, SHA-256 `d3de10283b9e5f666aae16d407800afbd65d2a7c2f094ee825de0847bcbc3f9d`. The OECD core list is fixed from `scripts/promote_oecd_pdb_batch03_2026_05_12.py`; the 28 countries with selected keys are AUS, AUT, BEL, CAN, CHE, CZE, DEU, DNK, ESP, FIN, FRA, GBR, GRC, HUN, IRL, ISL, ITA, LUX, MEX, NLD, NOR, NZL, POL, PRT, SVK, SVN, SWE, USA.

For each registered key, require finite productivity growth at `t`, and finite positive employment levels at `t-1`, `t`, and `t+1`. Only blank or `A` observation status is admitted. Growth-rate `UNIT_MULT` must be zero. Employment levels use the recorded base-10 multiplier before ratios. Exclude absolute productivity growth above 50 percentage points or absolute prior/forward employment growth above 50 log percentage points. Do not impute, interpolate, winsorize, change source, change sectors, or replace failed cases. Keep only countries with at least ten complete treatment years. Each cell then requires at least 450 country-years, 22 countries, and 20 years with at least 12 countries.

The fixed model is equal-row OLS of `100 ln(EMP[t+1]/EMP[t])` on OECD `GVAHRS` annual growth at `t` and `100 ln(EMP[t]/EMP[t-1])`, with country and treatment-year effects. Report a country-cluster CR1 standard error and two-sided Student `t(G-1)` p-value. A design is inconclusive if rank or estimator fails, residualized predictor SD is below 1.0 percentage point, residualized outcome SD is below 0.5 log percentage point, one country supplies more than 20% of residualized predictor sum of squares, or the top 1% of rows supplies more than 30%. Five country folds are assigned by `int(SHA256(ISO3)[0:8],16)%5`; each leave-fold-out fit must retain at least 320 rows and 17 countries.

All three p-values receive Holm adjustment at family alpha 0.05. A qualified directional result requires adjusted p≤0.05, signed coefficient magnitude at least 0.05 next-year employment log percentage points per one productivity-growth percentage point, and the same sign in at least four of five frozen folds. The opposite sign with the same gates is **REFUTED**; an estimable weaker result is **PARTIAL**; a failed coverage/design gate is **INCONCLUSIVE**. The three cells are not three independent replications. The source-lock commits must follow this registration commit and precede any selected-value access and result commit; the runner enforces the sequence and exact replay.

The selection follows review of older IESET productivity and employment hypotheses. The source and outcome differ from prior aggregate convergence and hours-reduction tests, but the family p adjustment does not cover archive-wide selection. Same-year productivity growth can reflect sector demand, hours, outsourcing, capital and measurement; a one-year-later employment outcome and fixed effects do not identify an automation or policy effect. All three records are descriptive public calibrations only if their evidence gates pass, with zero causal, policy, school, position and featured credit. The source carries OECD standard permissions requiring attribution; raw and country-level values stay local.
