# Finland policy and drift source correction — 2026-10-10

This bounded correction separates legal decisions and eligibility from cabinet
tenure, and withholds unsupported cumulative policy-axis steps. It changes
authored policy and movement metadata, not any hypothesis result or causal
estimate.

## Compulsory education

`fi_compulsory_education_extension_2021` now records Act 1214/2020 as issued
on **30 December 2020**, with advance application duties from **1 January
2021** and the principal age-extension and free-study provisions from **1
August 2021**. The law continues beyond Marin's 2023 cabinet end. Eligibility
turns on whether the student's basic education obligation ended after 1
January 2021; the first substantial group was spring 2021 grade-nine leavers,
mostly born in 2005. Birth year alone is not a complete eligibility rule.
The old transfer, spending-share, environmental-stringency, and labour-law
axis entries and eight broad inferred hypothesis links were removed. A
prior-school-leaver comparison is a design seed requiring individual
completion dates, pretrend checks, and pandemic adjustment, not an effect.
Sources: [Finlex Act 1214/2020](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2020/1214)
and the [Finnish National Agency for Education's implementation notice](https://www.oph.fi/fi/uutiset/2021/opetushallitus-tukee-oppivelvollisuuden-laajentamisen-toimeenpanossa-uusia).

## Family leave

`fi_family_leave_reform_2022` now distinguishes Act 28/2022's **14 January
2022** enactment from **1 August 2022** effectiveness. The previous statutory
allowance available for a two-parent birth was 105 maternity + 54 paternity
+ 158 shared parental days (**317**); the new structure is 40 pregnancy +
160 + 160 parental days (**360**). This is a 43-day increase in *available
paid entitlement*, not observed take-up, annual cost, or a causal employment
effect. The policy retains only a weak transfer-expansion axis. Its previous
unsupported EUR 100 million annual cost, old 18-day paternity count, and
eight generic inferred links were removed. Estimated birth date and actual
birth/adoption timing are needed to classify exposed families; adjacent
cohorts are not automatically exchangeable. Sources:
[Finlex Act 28/2022](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/saadoskokoelma/2022/28)
and the [Finnish Government's eligibility and leave summary](https://valtioneuvosto.fi/en/-/1271139/family-leave-reform-enters-into-force-in-august-2022).

## Marin movement and constructed drift

The parent movement's four positive axes were previously attributed to its
**2019** start. The cumulative map has no expiry mechanism for temporary
programmes. Its spending-level step is therefore withheld: Statistics
Finland reports general-government expenditure/GDP at **53.3% in 2019**,
**57.5% in 2020**, and back to **53.3% in 2022**. The temporary pandemic
spike does not establish a lasting, separate non-transfer spending step.
The transfer step is narrowed from moderate in 2019 to weak in **2022**,
attributed only to the lasting family-leave entitlement, not temporary
COVID payments or free school materials. Sources:
[Statistics Finland 2020](https://stat.fi/til/jmete/2020/jmete_2020_2021-12-17_tie_001_en.html),
[Statistics Finland 2022](https://stat.fi/en/publication/cl8lpycp1rrsw0cvznpi8aayr),
and the family-leave sources above. The weak transfer code represents a
targeted entitlement; it is not a measured national transfer-share increase.
Later Finnish movement steps, including the Orpo-era transfer code, still
affect the net line; a lasting entitlement does not imply that the net
transfer-axis score stays positive in every later year.

Environmental stringency's retained strong step is attributed to the
**2022** Climate Act rather than the 2019 programme. Energy-supply
security's retained moderate step is attributed to Gasgrid's **20 May
2022** ten-year LNG-vessel lease. Commercial LNG service began in 2023;
the 2022 step codes a diversification decision, not delivered gas flow.
Sources: [Finlex Climate Act 423/2022](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2022/423)
and [Gasgrid's lease announcement](https://gasgrid.fi/en/bulletins/gasgrid-finland-oy-and-excelerate-energy-inc-have-agreed-to-lease-an-lng-terminal-vessel-the-lng-terminal-vessel-exemplar-will-ensure-finlands-security-of-supply-while-also-serving-the-needs-of/).
The movement's note also corrects the cabinet chronology: [NATO records
Finland's accession on 4 April 2023](https://www.nato.int/en/news-and-events/articles/news/2023/04/04/finland-joins-nato-as-31st-ally), before [Orpo's government was appointed
on 20 June 2023](https://valtioneuvosto.fi/en/-/236553176/prime-minister-orpo-s-government-appointed).
