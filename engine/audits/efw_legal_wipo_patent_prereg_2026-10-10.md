# Fraser legal rights to resident patent filings — preregistration audit

This is one new prospective measured family, not a rehabilitation of a pre-existing weak rerun. Canonical specifications already cover several Fraser legal-rights outcomes and several resident-patent exposures; no exact Area 2 change to forward resident-patent-rate outcome was found. The registered claim is descriptive and has no causal, policy, school, position or featured credit.

No selected magnitudes, differences, signs, regression coefficients or verdicts were read before this registration.

## Frozen analysis and stop rules

Area 2 change from t-3 to t predicts forward change in log(1+resident applications per million) from t to t+3. Baseline log filing rate and log real GDP per capita, country effects and treatment-year effects are fixed controls. Equal-weight OLS uses country-cluster CR1 uncertainty and Student t(G-1). The sample is exact calendar-year joins, valid score 0..10, nonnegative integer-like filings, at least one million residents at both endpoints, positive real income, blank/A WIPO/WDI status, six complete years per country; no interpolation or substitute vintages.

The test is INCONCLUSIVE below 700 rows/65 countries; below 150 material Area 2 changes (|change|>=0.25) in 40 countries; on rank/uncertainty failure; above 0.15 maximum one-country or 0.25 top-one-percent residualized exposure share; or if any fixed country hash-fold omission falls below 500 rows/50 countries or cannot be fit. With gates clear, SUPPORTED requires beta>=+0.10 log points per +1 Area 2 point, p<=0.01 and at least four positive fold signs; REFUTED requires beta<=-0.10, p<=0.01 and four negative signs; otherwise PARTIAL. Hash-fold is SHA256 of ISO3, first eight hex digits modulo five. The family has one test and alpha 0.01; this does not correct selection across the visible IESET archive.

Source roles and byte hashes are fixed in the runner and will be committed in a **separate source lock after this registration**, before selected values are opened. The source lock will include the raw Fraser workbook, its derived panel and tracked parser, WIPO and WDI vintages and manifests. Exact byte replay of all result artifacts is required. Any parser-only repair after source access must be separately disclosed and may not alter the frozen design or gate thresholds.
