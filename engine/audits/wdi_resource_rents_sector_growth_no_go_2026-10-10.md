# WDI resource-rent sector-growth family: no-go audit

This note follows the key-only registration `157e1225b6868a5228d9dee3571a56aea9fb9be5`, eight separate ordered source locks, and measured result `07b5f716f5fc389810ff64ed5d20a7a46c69f9ec`. It changes no frozen source, sample, method, threshold, specification, result packet, or runner.

All eight registered cells are **INCONCLUSIVE and archived**. Complete paired-sector samples contained 495–509 country windows from 141–146 countries. Coverage floors passed, but the largest 1% of residualized rent observations accounted for **39.4%–61.2%** of the rent signal's squared variation, exceeding the preregistered 35% maximum in every cell. Oil, natural gas and mineral cells also exceeded the 25% maximum contribution from one country. The model therefore stopped before estimating an interpretable primary coefficient. Holm retains all eight cells with p=1. No support, refutation, policy effect, school score, or public calibration is claimed.

The source-key roster gave 860 possible rows per cell across 215 ISO3 national codes and four nonoverlapping anchors. WDI's dense country–year grid did not establish observed-value coverage. The difference between that envelope and the 495–509 complete rows, followed by concentrated producer-country variation, explains the no-go. A future design may prospectively target producers or use a different estimand, but this measured family cannot be repaired by changing its influence gate after seeing the values.
