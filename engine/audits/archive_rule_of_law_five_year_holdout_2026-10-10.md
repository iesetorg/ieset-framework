## Prospective design and custody

Registration `e3eacf6978bf8f52fbdcedea8a147729362d745e` fixed two scientifically distinct outcomes: annualized real GDP per person growth over exact non-overlapping five-year windows, and the mean published annual real gross fixed-capital-formation growth over the same forward windows. Both use WGI rule of law measured at t=2002, 2007, 2012 or 2017 with baseline income, investment share, trade share, dependency ratio, country effects, block-year effects and country-clustered uncertainty. The 131-country roster was fixed from source country/year keys only. It excludes the union of all 62 countries in the two earlier panels and 14 territorial or nonuniform geographies. Selected outcome magnitudes and null masks were not inspected before registration.

The two outcomes are **one family**, not two independent institutional replications. A two-sided p≤0.025 per test controls family-wise alpha at 0.05 by Bonferroni. Each outcome separately requires at least 280 complete blocks, 80 countries with at least three complete blocks, 50 countries whose observed rule-of-law range is at least 0.05, finite identified country-clustered uncertainty, and no country contributing over 15% of residualized treatment variation. Nonsignificant estimates with passed gates are `PARTIAL`; failed gates are `INCONCLUSIVE_DATA_PENDING`.

Full `validate_specs.py` passed **6,315** specification files and cross-references after the registration commit, with 28 already-permitted candidate policy forward references. `check_preregistration.py` passed with both new specs correctly listed as unrun before selected outcome access. Source lock `2614ed95857189d7494ff2335bab30d5c6426509` recorded seven exact WGI/WDI April 30–May 5 local-vintage hashes, roster hash and horizons, without selected values or null masks. Estimator commit `767697f265a81b978d6afdce5531cf5cfa9c1414` preceded the first data run. A key-only loader fix `87349a46932a8d98c62cf9efd0af6d3006fc0f42` restricted duplicate-key rejection to the frozen roster: the WGI file has 208 duplicate rows under a blank country code, but **zero** duplicate selected country/year rows. It changed no model, gate, sample rule or source.

Both result packets were saved in commit `3ad503500fca52f6972ec71baa4071532e239cb5`. Exact replay passed for each. An independent ordinary least-squares implementation with explicit country and block-year dummies reproduced the GDP-growth coefficient +1.21927 (clustered SE 1.01078, p=0.22772), and confirmed zero countries overlap either old panel. The preregistration check after result found 825 strict pairs, 1,320 legacy pairs and one unrelated unrun spec. A temporary evidence-tier build classified the GDP-growth ID as `calibration` with no exclusion and school-score eligibility false; fixed-capital growth remains `archive` with `non_result_verdict`. It computed **576 public / 2,146 total**, a **+1 public** change from the consolidated live count of 575 before this family. The two IDs count as one shared evidence family. Generated site indexes are intentionally left for the consolidated integration.

The government-effectiveness sensitivity was unavailable on the *same primary sample* because five GDP-growth rows lack a finite GE value; its failure did not alter the frozen primary verdict. This panel is associational. WGI ratings can react to growth, common institutions can affect both WGI and outcomes, and total fixed capital is not private investment. Neither result receives causal, school, policy, position or featured credit. The capital-growth no-go cannot be repaired by dropping four countries from the floor or by refitting a selected subset after seeing coverage; a future independent source or prospective version would be required.
