# Uncited exact-date policy repair wave — 10 October 2026

## Screen and decision

The source-presence screen found **313** policy candidates with a parsed full enacted, effective, or evaluation event date and no nonblank `references`. In the then-current policy-browser index, **0** had tested public-hypothesis coverage, **11** were related-public-only navigation, and **302** were archive-only. This is a triage count, not a claim that all 313 dates are wrong. Five prominent records have now been reviewed against primary or issuing-authority material. The uncited exact-date queue falls to **308**. Four of the five retain a source-backed full date; the Volcker bundle reverts to year-only timing because the recorded day was a decision and announcement rather than a common treatment onset.

## Deferred exact-date records

Seven of the originally related-public-only records remain uncited. The Italy Treasury–Banca d'Italia `divorzio` row presently treats a letter date as enactment; the 1973–1981 U.S. oil controls row compresses several statutes, allocation rules, and decontrol dates; and the France Maastricht row labels a referendum day as enactment. Turkey's low-rate cycle and governor-dismissal bundles also lack a single common enactment date. The 2018 Turkey lira/Brunson bundle uses 10 August for a mix of U.S. tariffs, sanctions, Turkish monetary response, and bank regulation, not a single Turkish enactment. Portugal's 1998 central-bank law has staged provisions, including an immediate section and later euro-integration changes. Each needs its own narrower instrument/event definition before a source citation should unlock exact-event readiness.
