## Frozen test and interpretation

Use one country/year fixed-effects OLS per cell: OECD employment ratio(t+1) − ratio(t) on WDI real GDP growth(t), with growth(t−1) as the fixed linear covariate. One GDP-growth percentage point is the treatment unit; one employment-rate percentage point is the outcome unit. Full country-cluster CR1 small-sample correction and two-sided Student t(G−1) inference. Outcome `value` must be finite in [0,100] with `OBS_STATUS` blank or A at both years. Do not substitute the duplicate `Observation value` column, interpolate, winsorize, exclude GDP shocks, or change age/sex bands. After invalid exact-roster pairs are dropped, retain only economies with ≥8 complete pairs.

All 20 tests stay in one Bonferroni family, alpha 0.01, so each two-sided p cutoff is 0.0005. SUPPORTED requires beta≥+0.10 employment percentage point per GDP-growth point and p≤0.0005; REFUTED requires beta≤−0.10 with the same p; all other estimable results are PARTIAL. Any source/chronology/sample/influence/rank/uncertainty failure is INCONCLUSIVE. After the lock, each cell requires ≥750 complete pairs, ≥40 economies, ≥20 distinct years, ≥500 material-growth rows across ≥35 economies; maximum 35% of partialled-growth variance in the top 1% rows, 20% in one economy, or 20% in one year; full-rank design and finite positive clustered SE. These floors were fixed before selected outcomes. Ireland's 2015 growth and other exposure outliers remain; the influence gate may make a cell INCONCLUSIVE. No secondary estimate can upgrade a verdict.
