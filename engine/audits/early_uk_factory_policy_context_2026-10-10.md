the 1802 and 1815 candidate policies. Ireland was part of that jurisdiction
in the cited years; neither policy should be separately duplicated into
modern `IRL` or read as a present-day UK statute. Each candidate suppresses
generic inferred axis links until a specific hypothesis has a defensible
claim-level match.
