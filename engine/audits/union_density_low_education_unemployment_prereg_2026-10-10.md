# Blind preregistration: union density and low-education unemployment

**Status: frozen design, before selected outcome values, null masks, or status fields are opened.** This is one prospective descriptive component of archived `union_density_wage_compression_employment_tradeoff`, one of exactly 131 old runs whose loaded treatment was `fraser_efw:summary_index` and whose loaded outcome and control both used `world_bank_wdi:NY.GDP.PCAP.KD`. The old union-density run reported a near-zero coefficient (+9.404764516e-17) with within R²=1, despite a tiny p value; that is an estimator failure, not valid support or refutation. The original multi-part ID and score mapping remain archived.

The selected outcome is OECD `DSD_LMS_low_education_unemployment_rate`, restricted to annual observed unemployment for both sexes, ages 25–64, ISCED11A_0T2, percentage of the labour force. Its `value`, `Observation value`, `OBS_STATUS`, and any null/status mask were not read during design. Only selected country/year keys and dimension labels were read. The treatment is actual-status A OECD `TUD`, percentage of employees, with finite values in [0,100]. A positive finite WDI real GDP-per-capita control at t was read. All three exact raw Parquets and their committed fetch-manifest entries are pinned by SHA in the registered spec and runner.

The frozen roster SHA-256 is `c8b551df75f431ab1450ecc7663f2b16d30b04dbd1c31c2a3d8151cf6b6c64cf`: 200 possible rows among 39 OECD-source reporter economies, not a count of OECD members. Anchors are 2007 (34), 2010 (36), 2013 (34), 2016 (37), 2019 (31), and 2022 (28); 35 reporters have at least three possible windows. Each outcome window is t→t+2, and windows within a reporter do not overlap. These are key-only counts; they do not certify a value or actual-status observation. No alternative anchor years, age groups, education groups, or outcome vintages will be substituted.

The primary estimand is the coefficient of TUD divided by ten in OLS for low-education unemployment(t+2) minus unemployment(t), controlling for unemployment(t), log real GDP per capita(t), country fixed effects, and anchor-year fixed effects. Country-cluster CR1 standard errors and Student t(G−1) two-sided p values are frozen. Before fitting, require at least 120 complete rows, 25 reporter economies, 20 economies with three nonoverlapping windows, five distinct anchors, 15 rows in every retained anchor, no anchor with more than 30% of the sample, union-density IQR at least 10 percentage points, and 20 economies with at least a three-point observed union-density range. After fit, require full rank, finite positive clustered uncertainty, top one percent of residualized treatment variation at most 20%, and largest country share at most 15%. A floor failure is INCONCLUSIVE and publishes no coefficient.

One hypothesis is registered in this family, with two-sided p≤0.01. SUPPORTED requires β≥+0.25 percentage point of two-year low-education unemployment change per ten union-density points; REFUTED requires β≤−0.25 with p≤0.01; an estimable weaker or nonsignificant result is PARTIAL. The threshold is local to this one calibration and does not account for the historical 131-claim search. No causal, school, policy, or featured credit can follow from it.
