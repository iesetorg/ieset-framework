## Peru replay boundary

`scripts/rerun_peru_post_fujimori_frozen.py --preflight` passed key-only checks: 190 fixed country-year keys each for GDP per capita, fiscal balance, and terms of trade, plus the 19 Peru CPI keys required by the rule. It did not load the `value` column. The runner and its test are uncommitted; a `--run` attempt stopped at the commit gate before value access and wrote nothing. Fourteen synthetic structural tests pass, but they establish only software behaviour, not that the chosen estimator is the frozen estimator. The historical top-level run files retain their recorded hashes.

**Independent methodological review: NO-GO.** The v1 estimator says “Peru-indicator interacted with year” without identifying a numeric linear trend, categorical year interactions, or another functional form. The draft selected a linear slope after the generic result was already visible. It also treats WGI as ancillary and absorbs the copper-price and US-rate controls under year fixed effects; the frozen text does not establish that this is the sole permitted treatment of those declared variables. The original rule prose requires fiscal dispersion **below** the peer median, while its threshold line permits equality. The claim invokes top-quartile growth and a five-administration mechanism without registered administration breakpoints. These ambiguities cannot be cured by a source hash, a passing preflight, a conservative calibration cap, or a historical snapshot. The current public page also does not visibly surface a corrective rescope.

Do not commit or run the draft as a frozen-v1 repair, and do not replace the canonical `engine/runs/peru_post_fujimori_growth_2001_2019/` files. Keep the old ID archived. A **new-ID prospective** registration must fix the time interaction, all outcome and control roles, comparator and temporal sample, complete decision rule, and visible result framing before any genuinely held-out values are fitted. The draft runner and tests remain uncommitted for audit only; their conditional promotion code is not authorization to execute it.
