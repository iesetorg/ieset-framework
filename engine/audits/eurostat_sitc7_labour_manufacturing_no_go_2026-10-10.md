# Eurostat SITC7 export share to later manufacturing and labour outcomes — source-key no-go

**Decision:** Do not preregister the proposed four-cell prospective family. This is a source-definition and value-blind feasibility decision; no selected numeric values, observation flags, null masks, associations, directions or results were opened. It adds no hypothesis, result, public calibration, policy credit or school credit.

The pinned official Eurostat `ext_lt_intratrd` candidate has `sitc06=SITC7`, `indic_et=PC_EXP_PROD` and `partner=WORLD`. SITC7 is **machinery and transport equipment as a share of goods exports**; it is not a measure of machinery manufacturing output or policy exposure. Its national source keys cover exactly 27 current EU countries over 2002–2025 (648 country-years). A t−2→t predictor paired with t−2/t/t+2 outcomes has an upper ceiling of 307 country-anchor keys across 27 countries and 17 years for 2005–2021, or 243 keys/27 countries/nine anchor years for 2012–2020, before reading numeric completeness. The local Eurostat parquets omit observation-status flags, limiting quality screening.

The four proposed endpoints do not form four cleanly distinct manufacturing consequences. Manufacturing real GVA (`nama_10_a10`, B1G/C/CLV_I20) and manufacturing hours (`nama_10_a10_e`, EMP_DC/C/THS_HW) can be combined into a productivity ratio, but that third outcome is mechanically determined by the first two. The pinned labour-productivity source (`nama_10_lp_ulc`, RLPR_HW/I20) lacks a `nace_r2` dimension: it is whole-economy productivity, which avoids exact arithmetic duplication but no longer answers a manufacturing-specific question. Unemployment (`une_rt_a`, T/Y15-74/PC_ACT) is an economy-wide labour-market measure. Existing authored EU single-market and trade-to-manufacturing-productivity work also limits novelty. Four nominal cells would overstate distinct evidence from this small EU-only panel.
