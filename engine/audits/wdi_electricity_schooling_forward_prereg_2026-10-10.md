## Frozen estimand, exclusions, and model

For every anchor t=2000–2018, exposure is `(electricity access(t) − electricity access(t−5))/10`, in ten-percentage-point units. Outcome is the exact t→t+3 percentage-point change. Five outcomes: primary completion, secondary net enrolment, tertiary gross enrolment, adult literacy, and adult upper-secondary attainment. WDI indicator keys are fixed in each YAML. Require blank or `A` WDI observation status, finite electricity in [0,100], positive finite real GDP per person, and finite outcome endpoints in [0,150] primary completion, [0,100] secondary net/adult literacy/upper-secondary attainment, or [0,200] tertiary gross. No imputation, winsorization, alternative proxy, outcome-contingent country selection, or neighboring-year join. Retain countries with at least three complete pairs.

Fit equal-pair OLS of the three-year outcome change on earlier electricity gain, baseline outcome at t, electricity access at t−5, log real GDP per person at t, prior five-year log income growth, and country and anchor-year fixed effects. Use CR1 country-cluster uncertainty and two-sided Student t with G−1 country degrees of freedom. The overlapping annual windows remain correlated within country; clustering addresses within-country dependence in the reported uncertainty but does not make this causal.

A case is **INCONCLUSIVE** before estimation if fewer than 400 complete pairs, 50 economies, or 10 anchor years; fewer than 50 ≥10-point material-gain pairs or 20 material-gain economies; or fewer than 100 ≤2-point low-change pairs or 30 low-change economies. After the complete-case filter, require full model rank and positive residualized exposure variance. The largest 1% of squared residualized exposure may contribute at most 20%, one economy 15%, and one anchor year 20%. Require finite positive country-cluster standard error. Any gate failure admits no public coefficient. These gates stay fixed after outcome access.

The five tests stay in the Bonferroni denominator even if one fails. Family α=0.01; each two-sided directional cutoff is **p≤0.002**. SUPPORTED requires a positive slope at least the outcome-specific practical magnitude; REFUTED requires an equally large negative slope; all other estimable cases are PARTIAL. Practical magnitudes per +10 access points are: primary completion 0.5 point; secondary net enrolment 0.5; tertiary gross enrolment 1.0; adult literacy 0.3; adult upper-secondary attainment 0.5. The local five-test correction does not erase archive-wide search.

## Proxy quality and publication boundary

National electricity access is an estimated population share, not school electrification, grid reliability, hours, affordability, or appliance ownership. UNESCO educational series have different populations and can share survey or administrative inputs. Adult literacy and attainment stocks can move slowly; tertiary gross enrolment can exceed 100. Development, urbanization, education budgets, institutions, migration, and reporting can confound all five pairings. The five outcomes are correlated and must not be described as independent replications. Every verdict gets **zero causal, policy, school-score, position, and featured credit**. Only a gate-cleared descriptive calibration may be public.
