# Internet uptake and performed public R&D to resident patenting: prospective registration

This registers **two** new, source-measured descriptive association tests. The outcome is the exact `t` to `t+2` change in `log(1 + resident patent applications per million residents)`, using the WIPO-origin resident application indicator transported through the World Bank API and WDI de facto population. The two prespecified exposures are (1) the WDI percentage-point change in Internet use from `t−2` to `t`, and (2) the Eurostat sum of R&D **performed** by government and higher-education sectors as a share of GDP at `t`. Sector of performance is not a measure of government funding. The preexisting 35-country European roster and treatment years 1998–2017 give patent outcome years through 2019, before the COVID-19 disruption.

The earlier IESET business-enterprise R&D to resident-patent test and older innovation/patent candidates were visible before this family was selected. Neither exact directed exposure-to-patent pair exists in the current hypothesis corpus. A WDI high-tech-export-share candidate was screened using treatment values only and rejected: its 2007–2017 panel had 374 treatment-valid rows, with Iceland carrying 30.8% of country/year-residualized exposure variance and the top 1% of rows carrying 33.2%. That candidate was not given a patent outcome test. This selection history means the Bonferroni correction below is local to the two frozen outcome tests and does not control the adaptive corpus search or provide independent replication.

Before this registration, the WDI Internet-use and Eurostat R&D **treatment** values were inspected to establish feasible exact-year keys, within-country variation, and concentration. The candidate patent, population, and real-income files were read only for `country_iso3`, `indicator_id`, and `year` keys. No patent, population, or income numeric values, status codes, or null masks were opened. The WIPO-origin patent file has a full 35-country/year key grid; that key-only upper bound says nothing about complete measured coverage. The exact frozen roster has 684 Internet rows/35 countries and 620 performed-R&D rows/34 countries after the five-treatment-year floor. Internet has 410 rows/35 countries with an absolute two-year uptake change ≥5 percentage points; performed R&D has 34 countries with a within-country range ≥0.05 GDP percentage points. Treatment-only residualized exposure concentration is below the prespecified 30% top-row and top-country limits in both cases. Each source's raw SHA-256 matches its tracked fetch manifest.

The registration spec, both steelmen, this plan, the runner, and the exact key roster must be added together in one Git commit with **no measured results**. A narrow hash-checked source lock must be created and committed separately afterward. Only then may the frozen runner read patent, population, income values or their null/status masks; all results, including inconclusive or adverse ones, must be written without changing the registration. The runner checks first-addition commits, immutable registered files, source hashes and manifest custody, source-lock chronology, and exact replays of every stored result artifact. Raw vintages are ignored by Git; replay elsewhere requires the same bytes.

## Frozen estimands and gates

| Case | Exposure unit | Key-only ceiling | Complete-case floor | Material/variation floor | Practical absolute beta |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Internet uptake | 1 percentage point in `IT.NET.USER.ZS_t − IT.NET.USER.ZS_(t−2)` | 684 / 35 | ≥400 rows / ≥25 countries | ≥150 rows and ≥20 countries with absolute uptake ≥5 points | ≥0.005 log patent units per point |
| Government+HE performed R&D | 1 GDP percentage point of `GOV + HES` at `t` | 620 / 34 | ≥350 rows / ≥25 countries | ≥20 countries with ≥0.05-point within-country span | ≥0.25 log patent units per point |

Every retained country needs at least five complete `t` years. Both primary models use country and treatment-year effects, baseline log patent applications per million at `t`, and log real GDP per capita at `t`. Country-clustered CR1 uncertainty uses Student `t(G−1)` inference. A rank-deficient fit, nonfinite uncertainty, failed sample/material floor, or residualized treatment concentration above 30% in the top 1% of rows or any one country is **INCONCLUSIVE**. Otherwise a positive coefficient meeting both the case's practical magnitude and two-sided Bonferroni p≤0.005 **SUPPORTS** the narrow association; a negative coefficient meeting both **REFUTES** it; any other estimable result is **PARTIAL**. The family alpha is 0.01 across both tests, including an inconclusive test. No covariate or vintage switch follows the measured result.

The 2008–09 financial crisis remains within the primary window and can be a shared disturbance. Internet-use methodology, Eurostat sector coding, R&D and patent timing, domestic versus foreign filings, filing-law changes, population estimates, GDP denominators, and reverse causality limit interpretation. Neither estimate identifies a policy effect. Both have zero causal, policy, school, position, and featured credit regardless of statistical verdict.
