# French policy exact-date source wave — 10 October 2026

## Scope and count

Eight existing French **candidate** policy records with full enacted dates and no nonblank `references` were checked against the original law text at Légifrance. In this checkout, the policy-spec source-presence screen falls from **295 to 287** uncited full-date records. This is a provenance repair, not a measured policy effect, a new hypothesis test, or a hypothesis graduation. IDs and candidate status remain unchanged.

The enacted dates in all eight records were correct. Their dates refer to promulgation; publication followed in the *Journal officiel*. Seven omnibus or phased records now say `treatment_onset_unresolved: true`. This retains their verified legal dates while withholding the event-treatment registry's automatic treatment-timing readiness until a provision, treated population, comparison and exposure date are specified. The marriage law has a single source-backed legal commencement date of 19 May 2013, but no economic outcome or axis claim.

The remaining inferred links in Loi Macron and Loi Travail are still navigation only, not causal findings.

## Verification boundary

`python3 scripts/validate_specs.py` validated all 6,563 specs with the repository's 28 permitted candidate forward references; no schema or cross-reference errors were reported. The policy-browser, policy-evidence and event-treatment indexes are generated artifacts and are left for the integration owner to regenerate after merging this branch. The source-presence count is a direct scan of policy YAML files; generated indexes still show their pre-merge state.
