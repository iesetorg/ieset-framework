This bounded review started from integration commit `483f5122920ee4b66a233eecd920d951162544fc`. It checked nine existing candidate policy records against New Zealand's published Acts and one issuing-ministry review, and added one statute missing from the policy database. The source corpus changes from **3,427 to 3,428** policy records: the old, mistimed `nz_three_waters_repeal_2023` ID is replaced by `nz_three_waters_repeal_2024`, and `nz_trial_periods_all_employers_2023` is new. All ten remain candidate policy context. None is a tested hypothesis, observed economic outcome, or policy-effect estimate.

All ten policy records have explicit official references, empty inferred hypothesis links, and `treatment_onset_unresolved: true`. A legal date alone does not define a comparable treated population, actual application of a phased or optional rule, or a measured result. ## Drift attribution

`scripts/compute_country_drift.py` reads movement `axes_summary` entries, not policy `axes_moved`. The Ardern parent movement's environmental-policy step is now attributed to **2019**, the year of the Zero Carbon Act, instead of the 2017 government start. Its monetary-expansion step is attributed to **2020**, when the [RBNZ announced LSAP on 23 March and began purchases on 25 March](https://www.rbnz.govt.nz/financial-markets/domestic-markets/domestic-markets-media-releases-listing-page/reserve-bank-to-begin-large-scale-asset-purchases---23-march-2020). Its never-operative Fair Pay Agreement flexibility step and its Three Waters competition step are withheld. The Luxon parent movement's flexibility step is anchored to the **2023** trial-period statute rather than FPA repeal; its water-repeal competition step is withheld. Its central-bank-independence step is also withheld: the [RBNZ Economic Objective Amendment Act 2023](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2023/64/en/latest/) changed the policy objective, not legal or operational independence. The 2017 New Zealand composite value changes from **−3 to −12** because 2019/2020 decisions are no longer backdated. The 2026 composite remains **−17** after the signed-step corrections offset. The country map still spans **145 countries, 21 axes, and 1789–2026**. These are constructed policy-position values, not measured policy outcomes.

Other Ardern/Luxon parent axes, including fiscal spending, retain their existing coding and may still need instrument-level timing review. This wave does not imply that a policy record or exact event date graduates a hypothesis. The remaining 28 candidate movement-to-policy forward references are outside this source wave.
