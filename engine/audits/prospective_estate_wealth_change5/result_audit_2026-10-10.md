## Source and interpretation boundary

The [OECD classification](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/revenue-statistics-2025_3a264267-en/full-report/the-oecd-classification-of-taxes-and-interpretative-guide_c852c459.html) defines category 4300 as estate, inheritance and gift taxes and states that corrective refunds reduce reported net revenues. The pinned treatment is that category's revenue as a share of total tax revenue. It measures collections, not the legal tax rate or marginal inheritance-tax burden. The [WID methodology](https://wid.world/methodology/) identifies the equal-split adult top-1 net-personal-wealth share, and the local adapter documents the exact `shwealj992`/`p99p100` slice. WID estimates may be revised or interpolated. The five-year offset does not rule out reverse causation, asset-price effects, death composition or changing enforcement.
