# V-Dem/WDI distinct-mechanisms preregistration — 2026-10-09

This is a two-test descriptive calibration family. The registration commit precedes opening any numeric V-Dem or WDI outcome values for these tests. No causal policy inference or position/school-score credit is permitted. Both results, including null, contrary, or data-limited results, must be retained.

## Frozen sample and estimands

The source-key-only V-Dem/WDI join contains 10,816 country-years across 175 ISO3 codes. Removing every country explicitly sampled by an earlier strictly verified hypothesis that mentioned a V-Dem source leaves the 61 countries written verbatim in both specifications. Their 2000–2023 source-key upper bound is 1,453 country-years; this is not a complete-case count. Older broad WGI and Heritage studies can still contain overlapping countries and years, but the two exact V-Dem measure/outcome pairs have no prior registered test. In particular, the Heritage property-rights/female-participation cross-sections use 2024; this family stops at 2023. The unrestricted countries may be reported only as a labeled sensitivity, never substituted for the registered sample or counted as a second hypothesis.

Each country contributes at most one observation. The V-Dem treatment is the mean of 2000–2004, standardized by the complete-case country standard deviation. The baseline outcome and constant-2015-dollar GDP per capita are means over 2000–2004. The late outcome is the mean over 2019–2023. Female labor-force participation uses late minus baseline percentage points. Under-five mortality uses log(late mean) minus log(baseline mean); its baseline control is log(baseline mean). OLS includes an intercept, treatment, baseline outcome, and log baseline GDP per capita with HC3 standard errors. At least three finite observations in each required five-year source window, 35 complete countries, and five distinct exposure means are required. No imputation or post-registration proxy substitution is allowed.

The family-wide two-sided alpha is 0.05, divided equally: each directional verdict requires a two-sided HC3 p-value at or below 0.025. The opposite significant sign is REFUTED, an estimable nonsignificant result is PARTIAL, and a failed data or model gate is INCONCLUSIVE. No results from the old corpus are used to select signs or thresholds.

## Distinctness audit and exclusions

The 61-country exclusion was frozen from the verified V-Dem-source inventory before outcome inspection. No earlier hypothesis specification or run uses `v2cldmovew` or `v2dlencmps`. Existing generic rule-of-law/corruption/regulatory-quality studies test many labor, health, investment, and growth outcomes, so this family does not re-label those generic governance predictors. The rejected expansion candidates were public-sector corruption → tax revenue and horizontal accountability → fiscal balance: their WDI fiscal series have only 5,669 and 5,218 non-null rows in their full global vintages, and the corruption/tax pairing is close to an existing reverse-direction holdout. A 10–20-test family would reuse semantic aliases and is not registered.

Source metadata and country/year keys were inspected before this registration; measured predictor, outcome, and control values were not opened or fit. The local isolated checkout lacks the V-Dem raw panel and codebook and two WDI outcome files at registration time; those exact hash-matching ignored vintages are available in the primary checkout for later custody recovery. Their recovery and verification must be recorded in a separate source-lock commit before results.
