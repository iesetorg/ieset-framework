# National central-bank independence timing audit — 10 October 2026

## Boundary and decision rule

The Maastricht scope repair removed a treaty-wide 1992 movement from national
scores. This follow-on review examined the remaining European
`monetary.central_bank_independence` steps, especially national movements whose
start year preceded a cited domestic law, exchange-rate pegs, euro entry, and
later duplicates of an existing autonomy change. A movement contributes to the
country drift map; a policy-only record supplies context and does not contribute
a second map step. A peg, monetary-target announcement, accession plan, or ECB
interest-rate decision is not itself an increase in a national central bank's
independence from its fiscal authority.

This is a bounded correction to authored coding, not a claim that every
historical independence assertion has been re-reviewed. National fiscal and
market axes in the same movements were retained. The map still derives its
earliest year from the 1789 national movement; no unsupported pre-1789 value was
inserted.

score in **2005–2026** that was based on ECB rates. Its composite delta is
**+3** in 1974–86, **+6** in 1987–2004, and **+4** in 2005–2026. Its
1948 independence step is unchanged. Across the map, **10 countries** and
**351 unique country-years** change: **351 CBI axis values**, **22 monetary
expansion axis values**, and **351 composite values**, or **724 derived values**.
All other country-axis and composite values, including every fiscal axis,

The first coded signed CBI year moves from **1970 to 1998** for Austria,
**1992 to 1993** for Belgium, **1982 to 1994** for Spain, **1991 to 1998**
for Finland, **1996 to 1997** for Greece, **1999 to 1998** for the Netherlands,
and **1985 to 1998** for Portugal. The generated grid remains **1789–2026**,
with **145 countries** and **21 axes**. First-year metadata is a coding boundary,
not evidence of zero policy before that year.

candidate forward references. Focused Maastricht and central-bank timing tests
passed, and the drift generator check matched the isolated regenerated output.
