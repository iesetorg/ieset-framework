# Labour-margin decomposition diagnostic

For a common Eurostat LFS country, year, citizenship, sex, and age cell, the unemployed share of that cell's population equals its activity rate minus its employment rate. This identity helps interpret the two preregistered outcome families, but it supplies no third independent observation or hypothesis. No unemployment-share significance test or verdict is registered or counted here.

The 64 frozen activity and employment fits can use different complete-case country-years because the pinned Eurostat files have different missingness. Their reported coefficients must **not** be subtracted and described as an estimated unemployment-share coefficient. A common-sample contrast would be a new exploratory diagnostic with no graduation credit.

The 64 observed results were all PARTIAL under the one preregistered Bonferroni family cutoff, with 42 positive and 22 negative point estimates. This sign count is descriptive and does not establish independent replication or a policy effect. The pinned Parquet files omitted Eurostat quality/break flags, and public release still needs data-rights review for the underlying ITU Internet series.

Definitions: [Eurostat detailed annual LFS metadata](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/en/lfsa_esms.htm) and [World Bank Internet-use metadata](https://databank.worldbank.org/metadataglossary/world-development-indicators/series/IT.NET.USER.ZS).
