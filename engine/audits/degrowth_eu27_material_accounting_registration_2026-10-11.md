## One frozen test

- **ID:** `eu27_material_accounting_decoupling_gap_2016_2026_forward`; one hypothesis, one directional Degrowth claim, one primary comparison; family size 1.
- **Prediction:** among the fixed 27 EU countries with `real GDP per capita(2026) > real GDP per capita(2016)`, the share with lower direct-mass DMC per capita in 2026 will exceed the share with lower consumption-based RMC per capita by more than 10 percentage points.
- **Roster:** Austria `AT`, Belgium `BE`, Bulgaria `BG`, Croatia `HR`, Cyprus `CY`, Czechia `CZ`, Denmark `DK`, Estonia `EE`, Finland `FI`, France `FR`, Germany `DE`, Greece `EL`, Hungary `HU`, Ireland `IE`, Italy `IT`, Latvia `LV`, Lithuania `LT`, Luxembourg `LU`, Malta `MT`, Netherlands `NL`, Poland `PL`, Portugal `PT`, Romania `RO`, Slovakia `SK`, Slovenia `SI`, Spain `ES`, Sweden `SE`. EU/EA aggregates are excluded. All 27 must have complete 2016 and 2026 values; at least 20 must meet the GDP-growth condition.
- **Sources and selectors:** Eurostat `env_ac_rme` for RMC (`freq=A`, `indic_env=RMC`, `material=TOTAL`, `unit=T_HAB`); `env_ac_mfa` for DMC (`freq=A`, `indic_env=DMC`, `material=TOTAL`, `unit=T_HAB`); `sdg_08_10` for real GDP/person (`freq=A`, `na_item=B1GQ`, `unit=CLV20_EUR_HAB`). Select the exact two years and roster. Preserve raw publisher responses and metadata, exact query URLs, flags, UTC, license and SHA-256 in the source manifest before opening values.
- **Rule:** for every GDP-growing country, `d = 1[DMC2026 < DMC2016] - 1[RMC2026 < RMC2016]`. The primary gap is mean `d`; resample the complete country pairs 10,000 times with NumPy PCG64 seed `20261011` and a two-sided percentile 95% interval. Support requires interval lower bound `> +0.10`; refutation requires upper bound `<= +0.10`; otherwise inconclusive. Source or sample gate failures are `INCONCLUSIVE_DATA_PENDING`. No weighted, subperiod, or second scored comparison is allowed.
- **Vintage:** take the first jointly complete Eurostat retrieval on or after 2027-01-01. Do not wait for a favorable revision. If no eligible joint source vintage exists by 2030-01-01, record a data-pending result. Eurostat's metadata says RMC country data are normally updated in July and November; a 2027 release is an expectation, **not a confirmed date**.

## Interpretation and custody limits

Eurostat [RMC metadata](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/EN/env_ac_rme_esms.htm) defines raw material consumption as embodied extraction for final demand and says country estimates may be model-derived and do not add to the EU aggregate. [DMC metadata](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/en/env_ac_mfa_sims.htm) defines direct domestic material use; [real GDP/person](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/product/view/sdg_08_10) is chain-linked volume. This paired accounting contrast is descriptive. It does not identify the effect of offshoring, prove material sufficiency, or test any degrowth policy.

The selectors above were verified from Eurostat's official [RMC data structure](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/sdmx/2.1/datastructure/ESTAT/ENV_AC_RME), [DMC data structure](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/sdmx/2.1/datastructure/ESTAT/ENV_AC_MFA), [GDP data structure](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/sdmx/2.1/datastructure/ESTAT/SDG_08_10), and their referenced codelists on 2026-10-11. In particular, `sdg_08_10` contains both the `freq` and `na_item` dimensions, `B1GQ` names GDP at market prices, and `CLV20_EUR_HAB` names chain-linked 2020-euro per capita volume. This structural check did not request observation values. Recheck selector labels before the eventual run; a changed definition triggers the frozen data gate.
