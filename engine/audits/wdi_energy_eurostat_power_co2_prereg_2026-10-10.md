## Fixed questions and physical interpretation

1. **Renewable output share.** Across the selected 29 countries and treatment years 1991–2021, does a larger WDI `EG.ELC.RNEW.ZS` annual percentage-point rise from t−1 to t predict a **lower** Eurostat public electricity and heat-production CO2 log change from t to t+1? WDI's [indicator definition](https://databank.worldbank.org/metadataglossary/world-development-indicators/series/EG.ELC.RNEW.ZS) says this is renewable-generated electricity divided by all generated electricity, sourced from IEA. It is a generation **share**, not installed capacity or a renewable policy indicator.
2. **Electric-power use.** Across the same selected 29 countries and treatment years 1991–2022, does a larger WDI `EG.USE.ELEC.KH.PC` annual log rise in electric-power consumption per capita from t−1 to t predict a **higher** forward CO2 log change? The [WDI metadata](https://databank.worldbank.org/metadataglossary/africa-development-indicators/series/EG.USE.ELEC.KH.PC) describe net power use after losses and own use. It is neither domestic generation nor a direct measure of power-plant fuel combustion.

The [Eurostat `env_air_gge` dataset](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/env_air_gge%24defaultview/default/table) reports EEA/UNFCCC source-sector inventories. The exact outcome selector is `freq=A`, `unit=THS_T` (**thousand tonnes**), `airpol=CO2`, `src_crf=CRF1A1A` (**public electricity and heat production**). The [EEA category guide](https://www.eea.europa.eu/publications/emep-eea-guidebook-2019/part-b-sectoral-guidance-chapters/1-energy/1-a-combustion/1-a-1-energy-industries/%40%40download/file/1.A.1%20Energy%20industries%202019.pdf) identifies category 1.A.1.a as public electricity and heat production. The outcome is the log change in **positive** reported sector CO2 from t to t+1. It is territorial inventory CO2, not GHG CO2-equivalent, all-sector emissions, electricity carbon intensity, or a direct welfare measure.

## Frozen estimator, thresholds, and disruption diagnostic

For each case, regress the one-year forward `log(CO2[c,t+1])−log(CO2[c,t])` on the single WDI treatment change from t−1 to t, with country and treatment-year fixed effects. Use OLS, country-cluster CR1 covariance, two-sided Student t with `G−1` degrees of freedom, and a 99.5% confidence interval. There are no covariates, alternative lags, data-driven sample windows, or automatic replacements. A rank-deficient model, nonpositive/invalid cluster SE, no residual treatment variation, or exceeded concentration gate is **INCONCLUSIVE**. After source lock, drop only positive-finite-outcome failures, then countries with fewer than ten complete years; keep zero and small exposure changes in the model.

An estimable primary without both directional passes is `PARTIAL`. If the primary fails a gate, or a directional primary cannot estimate the mandatory diagnostic, use `INCONCLUSIVE`. The pre-2019 diagnostic fits only frozen roster years `t≤2018`, excluding forward endpoints in 2020–2023, so pandemic/2022 energy-crisis observations cannot alone create directional support or refutation.
