The complete CPC-01 named-crop census contains 59 items, including nonfood cotton lint and unmanufactured tobacco, selected solely from 1995–2022 annual country/year keys with exact t−1,t,t+1 PPI availability, CCKP baseline/year keys, at least 1,300 item-country-year triples, and at least 55 countries. Each has temperature and precipitation exposure cases in **one 118-test family**. All 118 cases are visible in the [family results](cckp_faostat_weather_named_crop_ppi_results_2026-10-10.md) and their individual run packets.

The pinned FAOSTAT ZIP and local parquet match exactly on **159,721** selected item/index/annual rows, including keys, binary64 numeric values, flags, and unit fields; see the [archive check](cckp_faostat_weather_named_crop_ppi_archive_check_2026-10-10.json). This verifies the selected cache rows, not upstream collection or the untracked archive-to-parquet transformation.

Across the 59 items, the key roster supplies **129,232** potential item-country-years and the frozen gates retain **126,917** complete item-country-years (counted once per item before doubling for exposure tests). Attrition: **2,312** exceed the preregistered absolute prior or future PPI log-growth bound of 1.0; **2** have excluded `I` flags; **1** has nonpositive or nonfinite PPI. No row is lost at the five-year-country floor. The raw observed flag counts at input positions include `t:I=1` and `t+1:I=1`; all **126,917** admitted rows carry `E` flags at t−1, t, and t+1. Thus every fitted PPI input is `E`-marked, a material source-quality limitation even though the numerical sample and cluster gates pass. Per-item and per-role counts are retained in each diagnostics file.

All 118 measured cells are **PARTIAL**. None meets the frozen positive/negative magnitude and two-sided Bonferroni cutoff `0.01/118 ≈ 0.0000847458`; the smallest observed two-sided p-value is about **0.00322**, above that cutoff. Every cell passes the complete sample and material-exposure gates; the minimum retained country count is 55 and the largest observed top-country share of residualized focal exposure is about 8.49%, below the frozen 20% cap. These checks do not turn the associations into causal estimates.

FAOSTAT's annual single-item producer price index is a **nominal local-currency farm-gate selling-price index**, base 2014–2016=100. It is not farm income, real price growth, or food consumer-price inflation. Annual national climate averages miss crop zones and seasons; inflation, exchange rates, imports, demand, storage, policy, crop mix, and reporting can confound the panel associations. The 118 tests are correlated; the family correction does not adjust wider IESET research selection. The disposition is **descriptive calibration only** with zero causal, policy, school, position, or featured credit.
