# jst_investment_share_1920_1940_gdp_pc_growth_2026

**Measured verdict: PARTIAL.** This is one cell in a 20-test descriptive JST investment-share family. It grants no causal, policy, school, position, or featured credit.

Period: 1920_1940; anchors: 1920, 1925, 1930, 1935. The exposure is 100 times the JST investment-to-GDP ratio at each anchor. The outcome is gdp_pc_growth over the next five years. Complete sample: 15 countries and 58 country-anchor intervals.

Country-and-anchor fixed-effect slope: **-0.2237 outcome pp per 1 pp investment share**. Two-sided country-cluster p-value: **0.11384 (Holm adjusted 1)**. Gate failures: **none**.

Four outcomes are distinct measured series but share countries and one source. The five eras use separate year windows. This is one substantive family, not 20 independent replications. Investment shares are endogenous to expected growth and national accounts; the association cannot identify an investment policy effect.
