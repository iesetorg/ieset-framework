# jst_term_spread_2000_2020_unemployment_change_2026

**Measured verdict: PARTIAL.** This is one cell in a 12-test descriptive JST term-spread family. It grants no causal, policy, school, position, or featured credit.

Period: 2000_2020; anchors: 2000, 2005, 2010, 2015. The exposure is the JST long nominal rate minus the short nominal rate at each anchor. The outcome is unemployment_change over the next five years. Complete sample: 18 countries and 72 country-anchor intervals.

Country-and-anchor fixed-effect slope: **-0.6298 outcome pp per 1 pp spread**. Two-sided country-cluster p-value: **0.230529 (Holm adjusted 1)**. Gate failures: **none**.

Four outcomes are distinct measures but share countries and one source. The three eras use separate year windows. This is one substantive family, not 12 independent replications. Nominal yields encode expectations and risk premia; the association cannot identify a monetary intervention.
