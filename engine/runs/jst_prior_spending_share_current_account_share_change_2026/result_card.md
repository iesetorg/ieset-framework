# jst_prior_spending_share_current_account_share_change_2026

**Measured verdict: PARTIAL.** One cell in an eight-test descriptive JST fiscal-share family. No causal, policy, school, position, or featured credit.

Prior spending share is 100 × JST nominal government expenditure / nominal GDP at t−1; prior revenue share is a control. The response is current_account_share_change over t to t+5. Complete sample: 18 countries, 305 country-anchor intervals, and 18 anchors.

Country-and-anchor fixed-effect slope: **0.2110 outcome units per 1 pp prior spending share**. Two-sided country-cluster p-value: **0.0625483 (Bonferroni adjusted 0.500386)**. Gate failures: **none**.

This is a historical predictive association. Revenue, automatic stabilizers, wars, monetary regimes, expected growth, demographics, and the GDP denominator can confound it. The eight outcomes share a single source and panel, so they are not independent replications.
