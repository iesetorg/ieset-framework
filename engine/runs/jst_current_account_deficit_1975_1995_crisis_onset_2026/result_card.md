# jst_current_account_deficit_1975_1995_crisis_onset_2026

**Measured verdict: PARTIAL.** This is one cell in a 14-test descriptive JST current-account-deficit family. It grants no causal, policy, school, position, or featured credit.

Period: 1975_1995; anchors: 1975, 1980, 1985, 1990. The exposure is minus 100 times JST nominal current account divided by nominal GDP at each anchor (higher means a larger deficit). The outcome is crisis_onset over the next five years. Complete sample: 18 countries and 72 country-anchor intervals.

Country-and-anchor fixed-effect slope: **2.3514 outcome pp per 1 pp deficit**. Two-sided country-cluster p-value: **0.351677 (Holm adjusted 1)**. Gate failures: **none**.

Outcomes use distinct fields but share countries and one source. The three eras use separate year windows. This is one substantive family, not 14 independent replications. Current accounts are endogenous to growth, saving, and policy; the association cannot identify an external-financing intervention.
