# jst_prior_spending_share_gdp_pc_growth_2026

**Measured verdict: PARTIAL.** One cell in an eight-test descriptive JST fiscal-share family. No causal, policy, school, position, or featured credit.

Prior spending share is 100 × JST nominal government expenditure / nominal GDP at t−1; prior revenue share is a control. The response is gdp_pc_growth over t to t+5. Complete sample: 18 countries, 312 country-anchor intervals, and 18 anchors.

Country-and-anchor fixed-effect slope: **0.0550 outcome units per 1 pp prior spending share**. Two-sided country-cluster p-value: **0.0560113 (Bonferroni adjusted 0.448091)**. Gate failures: **none**.

This is a historical predictive association. Revenue, automatic stabilizers, wars, monetary regimes, expected growth, demographics, and the GDP denominator can confound it. The eight outcomes share a single source and panel, so they are not independent replications.
