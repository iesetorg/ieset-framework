# jst_mortgage_share_1890_1910_real_house_price_growth_2026

**Measured verdict: INCONCLUSIVE.** This is one cell in an 8-test descriptive JST mortgage-share family. It grants no causal, policy, school, position, or featured credit.

Period: 1890_1910; anchors: 1890, 1895, 1900, 1905. The exposure is 100 times JST mortgage loans divided by all nonfinancial private loans at each anchor. The outcome is real_house_price_growth over the next five years. Complete sample: 7 countries and 28 country-anchor intervals.

Country-and-anchor fixed-effect slope: **unavailable after frozen gate failure**. Two-sided country-cluster p-value: **not estimated**. Gate failures: **country_floor, country_anchor_floor, anchor_floor**.

The two outcomes use distinct fields but share countries and one source. The four eras use separate year windows. This is one substantive family, not 8 independent replications. Mortgage composition may respond to housing booms and lending rules; the association cannot identify a mortgage-policy intervention.
