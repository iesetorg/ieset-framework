# jst_mortgage_share_1975_1995_crisis_onset_2026

**Measured verdict: INCONCLUSIVE.** This is one cell in an 8-test descriptive JST mortgage-share family. It grants no causal, policy, school, position, or featured credit.

Period: 1975_1995; anchors: 1975, 1980, 1985, 1990. The exposure is 100 times JST mortgage loans divided by all nonfinancial private loans at each anchor. The outcome is crisis_onset over the next five years. Complete sample: 18 countries and 72 country-anchor intervals.

Country-and-anchor fixed-effect slope: **-1.5305 outcome pp per 1 pp mortgage share**. Two-sided country-cluster p-value: **0.202246 (Holm adjusted 1)**. Gate failures: **country_exposure_concentration**.

The two outcomes use distinct fields but share countries and one source. The four eras use separate year windows. This is one substantive family, not 8 independent replications. Mortgage composition may respond to housing booms and lending rules; the association cannot identify a mortgage-policy intervention.
