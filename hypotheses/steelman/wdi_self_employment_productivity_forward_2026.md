# Steelman — Self-employment share and later worker productivity

The directional claim is plausible only as a lagged descriptive association. The modeled self-employment share includes employers and own-account work and does not measure formal employment. Sector mix, capital deepening and GDP accounting can drive both series. Baseline adjustment and fixed country-hash omissions test fragility but cannot identify a causal mechanism. An opposite, null or gate-failed result is retained under the three-test correction, and no result earns policy or school credit.
