# Steelman: Manufacturing value added/GDP after a tariff decline

Cheaper imported machinery and components could raise domestic manufacturing value added; the share also depends on other sectors.

A favorable association would be consistent with cheaper imported inputs or equipment, but the exposure is an import-weighted average and may shift without any policy change. The 2000–2010 outcome trend and 2010 baseline are controlled; country-level omitted factors and reverse causation remain. A negative or null association is also plausible if domestic import competition displaces capacity or if successful sectors change import weights. Only the frozen descriptive verdict is reported.
