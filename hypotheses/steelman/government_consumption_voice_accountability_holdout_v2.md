# Steelman: government_consumption_voice_accountability_holdout_v2

Among countries excluded from the old state-planning screen, higher government final consumption as a share of GDP at year t predicts a lower World Governance Indicators voice-and-accountability estimate at exact year t+1, conditional on real income per person and country and treatment-year effects.

A larger government operating share can accompany more centralized discretion, while accountable democracies may choose a larger public-service sector.

Government consumption is not central planning; the WGI voice-and-accountability estimate is a perception composite, not an information-quality measure. Country and treatment-year effects plus baseline income leave confounding and possible reverse causation. A result in either direction is a measured association, not a policy effect.

The old PARTIAL result was already known; all 40 countries in that historical specification are excluded. The expected sign is fixed by the claim above. Support and refutation use the same two-sided 0.10/11 family threshold after a 400-pair/35-country complete-case gate and five pairs per country. Feasibility selection inspected null masks only, without outcome magnitudes or fits. No school claim receives score credit.
