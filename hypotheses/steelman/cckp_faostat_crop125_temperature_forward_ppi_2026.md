# Steelman: Cassava, fresh, temperature, and later producer prices

Unusual annual temperature departures may disrupt or signal reduced expected supply of Cassava, fresh and raise later farm-gate prices. The frozen positive prediction is compared within countries and common years, with prior price growth and the other departure controlled.

FAO reporting and estimated data also matter. A fitted coefficient remains an association, not a causal crop or price response.
