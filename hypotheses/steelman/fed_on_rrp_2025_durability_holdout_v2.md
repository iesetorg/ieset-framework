# Steelman: 2025 ON RRP decline durability

The earlier result observed a high ON RRP peak through 2023 and a large
decline by 2024-Q3. A persistent low level throughout 2025 would make that
description more durable. A rebound in at least half of the quarters would
weaken it. Calendar quarters, thresholds, and the 55-day minimum are fixed
before 2025 amounts are read.

The series measures aggregate daily ON RRP transactions in USD billions. It
does not itself show whether Treasury bill supply, money-market-fund choices,
interest-rate spreads, regulatory changes, or other factors caused the
amount. A supported result would therefore be descriptive only. The same
source vintage was already used for the older result, and its runner loaded
the file before applying its old date filter; the specific new 2025 values
were not inspected while drafting this rule.

The pinned local parquet has a SHA-256 match in the earlier run manifest, and
FRED documents the series and units. No fetch-time manifest for this local
slice was found. That chain identifies the exact local bytes and published
series, but does not independently document the original fetch transaction.
