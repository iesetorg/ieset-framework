# Steelman: regulatory quality and poverty headcount

A regulatory setting that supports investment may precede lower poverty. This test compares the WGI regulatory-quality estimate at t with the next-year $3.00-a-day (2021 PPP) poverty headcount ratio, conditional on real income, rule of law, country effects, and year effects.

Regulatory quality is perceptions-based and not a dated reform. Poverty observations can be sparse and reflect many other influences. Conditioning on income does not make the association causal; it can also absorb pathways of interest.

The old 40-country PARTIAL result is disclosed and its entire roster is excluded. The complete-case gate is fixed before values, and both expected and adverse directions face the four-test Bonferroni cutoff. No school claim receives credit.
