# government_consumption_electricity_access_geographic_holdout_v2: steelman

## Strongest case

A higher current-consumption share can coincide with less fiscal room for extending electricity connections. An exact next-year outcome in countries excluded from the old fit tests whether this measured association travels.

## Best contrary case

A high share may reflect recessions or public-service delivery while access is driven by grid geography and long-lived investment; the sign could reverse. Country and year effects do not establish a causal effect.

## Measurement boundary

Government final consumption share is not state monopoly, procurement practice, electricity investment, or project cost overruns; electricity access is only a coverage measure.
