# Steelman: regulatory_quality_physician_density_country_holdout_v2

The historical `health_system_entry_barriers_capacity` result was visible before this follow-up. The new test excludes its entire registered country roster and measures only the stated source pair.

Income, public medical training, migration, and disease burden can determine physician supply. General regulatory quality is not a health-sector licensing rule, and physician counts do not establish care access or quality.

A fitted association in either direction is limited to the named indicators, lag, fixed sample, and family threshold. It cannot establish a policy effect or earn school-score credit.
