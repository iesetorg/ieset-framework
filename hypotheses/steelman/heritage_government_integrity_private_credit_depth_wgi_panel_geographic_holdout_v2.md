# Steelman: heritage_government_integrity_private_credit_depth_wgi_panel_geographic_holdout_v2

The strongest version of this associational claim is that the annual control corruption wgi series captures a durable institutional channel and that its within-country variation relates to private credit depth beyond global year shocks, fixed country traits, income, demographics, trade, and government consumption. The countries omitted from the original 53-country panel provide a genuine geographic holdout for this limited association.

The strongest rival explanation is time-varying development or administrative measurement that shifts both WGI estimates and the outcome. WGI indicators are perceptions and can respond to the same economic conditions as the outcome. Income, trade, and fiscal controls do not remove every shared shock. The seven tests also share data and are correlated. A significant coefficient therefore cannot establish the effect of a policy intervention.

The frozen test includes every country with complete pinned data outside the old sample. A positive coefficient clearing the family-adjusted p threshold supports only the directional association; a negative coefficient clearing it refutes that direction; all other estimable results are partial. The old REFUTED result remains visible and receives no prospective credit.
