# Steelman — Rule of law and later homicide

The directional claim is plausible only as a lagged descriptive association. WGI rule of law is a perception-based composite that may itself incorporate crime and enforcement. Homicide reporting and social conditions also vary. Baseline adjustment and fixed country-hash omissions test fragility but cannot identify a causal mechanism. An opposite, null or gate-failed result is retained under the three-test correction, and no result earns policy or school credit.
