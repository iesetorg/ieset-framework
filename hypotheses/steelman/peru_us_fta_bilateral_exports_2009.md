# Steelman — US exports to Peru after the 2009 trade agreement

## Strongest case for the descriptive claim

The [US Trade Representative records](https://ustr.gov/trade-agreements/free-trade-agreements/peru-tpa) that the US-Peru Trade Promotion Agreement entered into force on 1 February 2009. Its [contemporaneous statement](https://ustr.gov/about-us/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2009/january/statement-us-trade-representative-susan-c-schwab-r) described new duty-free access for the vast majority of US exports to Peru. If lower border costs and greater legal certainty mattered for US suppliers, their exports to Peru could rise relative to exports from the same origin to nearby destinations without a US FTA. The primary outcome therefore measures **USA → PER**, not Peru → USA; much of Peru's access to the US was already covered by ATPA/ATPDEA preferences.

## Strongest case against the inference

1. **The comparator is imperfect.** Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Ecuador, Paraguay, and Uruguay differ from Peru in income growth, import demand, product mix, exchange rates, and non-FTA trade restrictions. The same US exporter and destination region remove some common shocks, but they do not make parallel counterfactuals.
2. **The 2008-2009 crisis and rebound overlap entry into force.** Dropping 2009 avoids mixing an eleven-month treatment year into either mean; it does not remove country-specific crisis effects or recovery paths.
3. **The agreement was anticipated and bundled.** It was signed and ratified before 2009 and coincided with domestic implementation, services, investment, labour, environmental, and other changes. Annual goods flows cannot isolate tariff cuts, anticipation, or those other channels.
4. **BACI is nominal merchandise value.** A rise can reflect prices, composition, demand, or exchange rates rather than a larger real volume. The data do not measure consumer surplus, firm productivity, wages, or welfare.
5. **FTA status is not treatment intensity.** CEPII `fta_wto` confirms a recorded agreement-status transition but does not measure preferential margins, tariff-line phase-ins, utilisation, or effective border costs. Some US products already had low tariffs.
6. **One treaty is one test.** The seven dyads are observations in a fixed comparison, not seven independent policy interventions. Reversing the direction or varying donors must not generate additional graduations from this treaty.

## Registered interpretation

The contrast is descriptive. It can support or refute the narrow directional flow claim only after exact source, coverage, treatment, and pretrend gates pass. A failed gate is inconclusive. Even a large positive contrast cannot establish that the TPA caused it. The result is calibration only and receives zero school score credit; any later causal claim needs tariff-line exposure, product-level outcomes, and an independent control design.
