# Steelman: Argentina's later core inflation persistence

The narrow favorable case is that lower inflation spread beyond headline CPI and persisted in the national core index from September 2025 through August 2026. A twelve-month median at or below 3.0% and at least nine individual months at or below 3.0% would show broad observed persistence, rather than one favorable print. The official INDEC national core series directly measures this component. Headline and services rates will be reported beside it, but cannot change the core verdict.

The strongest opposing case is that core inflation remains above 3.0% in most of the window or its median exceeds that mark. A mixed path with a qualifying median but fewer than nine qualifying months is explicitly partial. The result can be sensitive to exchange-rate movements, recession, administered-price changes, index composition, external prices, and lagged policy effects. A single country and a threshold description cannot separate those explanations or establish a government effect.

The old `milei_reforms_reduce_argentine_inflation` test registered headline-CPI windows ending in August 2025 and its May 2026 saved run was `INCONCLUSIVE_DATA_PENDING`. Its result card refers to a prior supported result that this repository lane could not reproduce; that result is neither adopted nor treated as unseen. This new ID uses a different CPI component and a later window; it does not repair or inherit the old verdict. The selected source was fetched before registration, so this is a registered analysis of existing data, with no claim that the values were globally unseen. No result will receive school-score or causal credit from this descriptive test.
