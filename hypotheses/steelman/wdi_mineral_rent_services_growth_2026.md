# Steelman: Mineral rents and later services growth

**Registered ID:** `wdi_mineral_rent_services_growth_2026`. Rent windfalls can raise local demand for nontradable services and finance service-sector investment. The registered five-year coefficient sign is positive after prior sector growth, baseline sector scale and income, country and anchor effects.

Countermechanisms are plausible: rent-funded infrastructure may increase manufacturing, while Dutch-disease pressures, volatile prices or state capture can dampen services. The rent estimates include a current-GDP denominator and the two sector series are national accounts with potential breaks. The four rent measures overlap, and eight cells share a Holm correction. This is a descriptive prospective test, not a causal estimate or policy recommendation.
