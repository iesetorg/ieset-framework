# Steelman: FTA-covered nearby market and private investment

Broader agreement-covered nearby markets may raise the expected scale of
tradable production and make private fixed investment more attractive.
The frozen exposure weights CEPII partner GDP by distance and asks whether
the following annual change in WDI private capital formation points in that
direction in countries outside the earlier IESET FTA-growth roster.

The alternative is that investor demand, macro stability and reforms select
countries into agreements. `fta_wto` records agreement status, not the
tariff cuts or actual access a firm faces; private investment shares can
move for domestic reasons or because the GDP denominator changes. The
correlated high-tech test shares this FTA source. An adverse or null result
counts under the same rule. Even a favorable result is descriptive only.
