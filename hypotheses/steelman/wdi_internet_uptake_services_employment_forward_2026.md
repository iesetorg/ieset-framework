# Steelman: wdi_internet_uptake_services_employment_forward_2026

## Claim and why it might be true

Observed diffusion of internet use can lower information and coordination costs. This case asks whether a 10-percentage-point larger three-year increase in internet-user share is associated with a later three-year rise in **services employment share** within a fixed global country-year key roster. The expected sign is positive. The exact selected measure is WDI `SL.SRV.EMPL.ZS`; the transformed outcome is WDI SL.SRV.EMPL.ZS percentage-point share at t+3 minus its share at t.

## Strong competing explanation

Income growth, trade integration, institutional changes, education, demographics, crisis recovery, and changes in WDI reporting can jointly move internet use and the selected outcome. Country and year effects plus baseline and prior-change controls reduce simple confounding but do not identify a causal effect. Internet-user share is an **uptake proxy**, not a policy intervention. WDI services employment is an ILO-modeled sector share; a larger share does not show better jobs or welfare.

## Fixed evidence and possible defeat

The 1,558-row, 196-ISO-economy 2009–2016 roster was formed from treatment, real-income, population values and selected-outcome **keys only**. WDI selected-outcome keys form a structural grid, so actual coverage is unknown before source lock. Complete-case floors require 400 rows/50 economies, seven treatment years, five years per retained economy, at least 60 material-rise rows/25 economies and 20 low-change rows/10 economies. Rank, country-cluster uncertainty, and residualized-exposure influence gates must also pass. A positive beta of at least 0.5 per 10-point uptake at two-sided p<=0.01/3 supports only a public descriptive calibration; a correspondingly negative beta refutes it. Any other gate-cleared estimate is PARTIAL and remains a public descriptive calibration. Gate failure is archived as INCONCLUSIVE.

This case belongs to the fixed three-endpoint `wdi_digital_diffusion_structure_2026_10_10` family. Earlier internet-use hypotheses and the internet-to-patent calibration make this retrospective test non-independent of corpus search. Local Bonferroni correction does not repair that archive-wide selection. Causal, policy, school, position, and featured credit are zero regardless of result.
