# Steelman — wdi_remittance_pre2015_forward_current_account_v2

Claim tested: Higher 2010–2014 remittance receipts as a share of GDP predict a higher 2020–2023 average current-account balance as a share of GDP across economies, after 2010–2014 current-account balance and 2014 real income are controlled. This is a descriptive forecast association, not a remittance-policy effect.

The strongest alternative is that migration and global income shocks change both remittance receipts and the outcome. Baseline adjustment and pre-outcome timing support a forecast comparison, but cannot identify a policy effect. The frozen two-test correction and leave-one-economy-out audit limit overstatement.
