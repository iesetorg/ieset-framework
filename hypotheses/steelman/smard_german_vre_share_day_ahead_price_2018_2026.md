# Steelman: German wind and solar generation and wholesale prices

Wind and solar have low short-run marginal costs. When their generation is
abundant relative to load, day-ahead wholesale prices may be lower even
within the same calendar year and season. The fixed test estimates that
co-movement using one monthly SMARD vintage and the continuous
Germany/Luxembourg price-zone period.

The strongest alternative is common cause and endogeneity. Weather changes
generation and demand; gas and carbon prices, imports, outages, storage,
curtailment and market conditions alter price. German generation/load is
not an exact measure of the Germany/Luxembourg bidding-zone balance. The
month and year indicators do not solve these problems. A significant slope
would be a descriptive association only, with no policy, causal, school,
position or featured credit. A null or reversed estimate is allowed.
