# Steelman: government consumption private credit depth holdout v2

Among countries excluded from the old state-allocation study, a higher general-government final-consumption share of GDP at year t predicts a lower private-sector bank-credit share of GDP at exact year t+1, conditional on baseline real income per person and country and treatment-year effects.

Government final consumption is not total state allocation, and bank credit is not the quality of private investment. Both are GDP ratios and may share denominator shocks. Country and year effects plus baseline income leave omitted causes and reverse causation. A result in either direction is only a measured association.

The old REFUTED result was known, and all 41 countries in that historical specification are excluded. The new direction is fixed by the claim above. Expected and adverse coefficients face the same five-test two-sided threshold of p<=0.02 after a 400-pair/35-country complete-case gate. No school claim receives score credit.
