# Steelman: fertility_decline_female_lfp_decade_forward_2026

A fertility decline can accompany economic modernization without causing female labour-force participation rate to change. Mortality, urbanization, education, migration, state capacity, and labour demand can move fertility and this outcome together. The selected WDI labour series are national ILO-modelled aggregates where relevant. Two anchor windows and country effects do not settle these alternatives. A weak, opposite, or gate-failing result must remain visible.
