# Secondary net enrolment rate: strongest case and limits

Public provision, teacher staffing, and fee relief may affect secondary-age participation. Net enrolment counts attendance at the intended age, not completion or learning quality.

The strongest interpretation is a broad education-system resource association, not an effect of a particular statute, financing formula, or school programme. Country-specific demand for schooling, teacher quality, demographic composition, and GDP-denominator changes can move spending and the outcome together. The registered three-year lag, controls, country/year effects, and six-test correction constrain interpretation but do not remove reverse causation or omitted changes. An inconclusive gate is retained rather than selecting a different lag or country subset.
