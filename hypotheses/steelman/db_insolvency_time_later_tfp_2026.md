# Steelman — resolution time and later routine productivity

A predictable insolvency system can preserve going-concern value and release assets or credit to productive uses. The registered contrast asks whether the publisher's resolution years measure precedes the specific later macro endpoint in a fixed country holdout.

The strongest rival explanation is common institutional and macroeconomic development: richer, more stable economies may both reform procedures and invest or grow. The Doing Business case is a standardized local scenario, not a census of firms; the historical series is retrospectively revised. Economy and year fixed effects do not remove time-varying credit, demand or political shocks. This result must remain descriptive and cannot validate the old post-crisis bailout claim.
