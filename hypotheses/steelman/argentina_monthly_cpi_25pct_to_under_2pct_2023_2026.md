# Steelman: argentina_monthly_cpi_25pct_to_under_2pct_2023_2026

Claim: Argentine monthly CPI fell from ~25.5% m/m in Dec 2023 to under 2% m/m by Aug 2026.

Strongest opposing case: the test is descriptive, not causal; confounders (cycle, energy-price shocks, compositional change, data revisions, preliminary prints) could produce the same pattern. Falsification: SUPPORTED if Dec-2023 m/m >= 20 AND latest (Aug-2026) m/m <= 2.5 AND mean of last 12 months <= 3.0; REFUTED if latest m/m > 4; PARTIAL otherwise.
