# wdi_female_upper_secondary_fertility_forward_2026 — steelman

Female secondary attainment can expand skills and reproductive autonomy and may predict later fertility decline. The age-25+ measure includes women outside current childbearing ages and may reflect cohort replacement. Total fertility is a period rate, not completed lifetime fertility. Fertility policy, urbanization, contraception, income and norms can change both series. An apparent association cannot identify an education effect. The education vintage has an exact raw hash and baseline pull declaration but no matching per-run fetch manifest, which limits provenance.

The test is descriptive and has zero causal, policy, school, position or featured credit.
