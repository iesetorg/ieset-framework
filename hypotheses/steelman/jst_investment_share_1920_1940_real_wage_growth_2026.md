# Steelman: jst_investment_share_1920_1940_real_wage_growth_2026

## Strongest case

A larger observed investment-to-GDP ratio can mark capital deepening and productive capacity. In the fixed 1920–1940 window, that may predict faster CPI-deflated wage-index growth over the next five years. The response is measured by wage,cpi; the predictor is JST iy, with country and anchor effects.

## Best objections and falsifiers

Investment may follow anticipated growth, and the GDP denominator may move independently. Business cycles, wars, structural changes, and imperfect historical data can create spurious association. Falsification is the frozen opposite-signed, Holm-significant effect with magnitude and country-deletion gates; weak effects or failed coverage remain PARTIAL or INCONCLUSIVE. Even a supported association would be descriptive and would not identify an investment intervention, policy effect, or school claim. The twenty correlated cells are one family and not independent replications.
