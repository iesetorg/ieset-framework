# Steelman: government_consumption_corruption_control_country_holdout_v2

The historical `discretionary_allocation_corruption_panel` result was visible before this follow-up. The new test excludes its entire registered country roster and measures only the stated source pair.

A larger public-consumption share may finance enforcement, health, and administration that improve corruption control. Both variables may respond to state capacity or income. An inverse coefficient is an association, not evidence that government spending causes corruption.

A fitted association in either direction is limited to the named indicators, lag, fixed sample, and family threshold. It cannot establish a policy effect or earn school-score credit.
