# Steelman: government_effectiveness_secondary_enrollment_country_holdout_v2

The historical `education_spending_vs_school_autonomy` result was visible before this follow-up. The new test excludes its entire registered country roster and measures only the stated source pair.

Families and schools can raise enrollment without a general government-effectiveness change, while school investment can shape governance perceptions. Gross enrollment is not learning quality or school autonomy.

A fitted association in either direction is limited to the named indicators, lag, fixed sample, and family threshold. It cannot establish a policy effect or earn school-score credit.
