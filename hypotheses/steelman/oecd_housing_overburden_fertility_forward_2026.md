# Steelman: oecd_housing_overburden_fertility_forward_2026

Housing cost pressure may delay births, but the OECD series measures only the low-income population while Eurostat total fertility covers all women. Income, migration, student debt, age composition, housing supply, and family policies can move both series. More expensive housing can coexist with higher fertility in growing regions. Within-country fixed effects and prior timing do not remove these alternatives. A weak, opposite, unstable, or gate-failing result must stay visible.
