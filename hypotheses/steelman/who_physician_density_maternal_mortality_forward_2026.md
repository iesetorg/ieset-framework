# who_physician_density_maternal_mortality_forward_2026 — steelman

Physician access can improve timely obstetric and emergency care, making a later maternal mortality decline plausible. Yet national density may include non-practising doctors and says little about distribution, referral networks, midwives, medicines or quality. The World Bank maternal ratio is modelled and contains socioeconomic inputs. Income, governance and reproductive health programmes can shift both series. The earlier OECD physician-density/amenable-mortality study makes this a selected distinct outcome, not an independent discovery.

The test is descriptive and has zero causal, policy, school, position or featured credit.
