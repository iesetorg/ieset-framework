# Steelman: jst_mortgage_share_1890_1910_real_house_price_growth_2026

## Strongest case

A larger mortgage share of all private loans can mark a financial system more exposed to residential collateral. In 1890–1910, it may predict higher real house-price growth over the next five years. The registered predictor is JST tmort/tloans; the response is hpnom,cpi. Country and anchor effects compare the fixed panel without changing the sample or horizon.

## Best objections and falsifiers

Credit composition may reflect current house prices rather than cause future prices or crises. Mortgage markets and accounting differ across countries and periods. A crisis code is an onset marker, not a measure of severity. The opposite-signed, Holm-significant effect with magnitude and country-deletion gates refutes the directional claim; weak effects are PARTIAL and failed coverage or concentration is INCONCLUSIVE. Even SUPPORTED is descriptive and gives no causal, policy, school, or featured credit. Eight correlated cells are one family.
