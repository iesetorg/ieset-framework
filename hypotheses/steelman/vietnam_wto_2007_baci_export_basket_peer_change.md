# Steelman — Vietnam WTO 2007 BACI export basket peer change

This is a one-case descriptive calibration. A favorable comparison would not establish that WTO accession caused export growth.

## WTO entry followed an existing export opening

The 2001 US-Viet Nam bilateral trade agreement, domestic market reforms and foreign investment preceded 2007. Viet Nam may already have been on a steeper export path than Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia and the Philippines. WTO approval and acceptance occurred during 2006, which is excluded along with 2007 to limit anticipation contamination. The frozen 2002–2005 pretrend gate is necessary but cannot rule out an accelerating prior trajectory or a different mix of goods.

## The donor origins are imperfect peers

The four ASEAN origins were already WTO members and had different industrial structures, exchange rates and domestic policies. An equal-weight nine-destination basket does not equal total exports or adjust for product mix. A median donor change can absorb some common destination demand but is not a constructed counterfactual. The donor-placebo rank has very coarse resolution and must not be reported as a conventional significance test.

## A global shock sits inside the post window

The 2008–2009 financial crisis affects every origin and destination, but can affect their export baskets by different amounts. Nominal BACI values also move with prices and exchange rates. Both 2006 and 2007 are excluded from the contrast because membership was approved and accepted in 2006, began 11 January 2007, and the annual CEPII flag cannot measure the exact tariff schedule.

## Source completeness is narrower than interpretation

The source has one existing-country dyad-year and an observed BACI field for all 495 frozen cells, with no CEPII-coded FTA among these origins and destinations during 2002–2012. That establishes coverage, not substantive comparability; preferential access outside the coded FTA flag, non-tariff policy, reporting revisions, and source construction can still change flows. A source or pretrend failure is INCONCLUSIVE. A result can be used only as a descriptive calibration, with no policy, causal, school, or featured score.

## Prior related work

The corpus already contains a China WTO manufacturing-export hypothesis and a broad WTO productivity-spillover hypothesis. The present endpoint is Viet Nam's directed nominal merchandise flow to a fixed destination basket, not TFP, real output, or a China replication. Seven earlier CEPII FTA-event calibrations yielded six pretrend failures; no success should be inferred for this WTO case from those related tests.
