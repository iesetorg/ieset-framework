# Steelman: us_federal_interest_share_of_receipts_doubled_since_2002

Claim: Interest on the US national debt now takes about 25% of federal tax revenue, up from about 12% in 2002.

Strongest opposing case: Interest share of revenue is a cyclical measure: it was depressed in 2002-2021 by unusually low rates, and the 1980s-90s saw comparable shares. A higher share is a fiscal-space constraint, not by itself evidence of an unsustainable path.

This test is descriptive, not causal.

Falsification (frozen): Share = FYOINT/FYFR. SUPPORTED if latest FY share >= 22% AND FY2002 share <= 15%. REFUTED if latest FY share < 15%. PARTIAL otherwise. Note: FYOINT is net interest; the post's $1.3T figure is gross interest including intragovernmental trust-fund interest, which this test does not use.
