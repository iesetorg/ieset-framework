# Steelman: Lack of social support after household debt-service change

A rising aggregate debt-service ratio can reduce the resources and flexibility of borrowers. The specified adverse-oriented outcome is lack of social support; the fixed one-year ordering, exact country/year roster, and ten-test correction give the prediction an opportunity to fail.

The strongest alternative is that Surveyed social support may be pooled across years and does not identify indebted respondents. Debt service itself moves with rates, wages, employment, and credit supply. The country series never links individual debtors to individuals reporting the outcome. An opposite sign, weak estimate, missing endpoint, concentrated exposure, or invalid cluster uncertainty must remain visible. This descriptive association cannot establish a debt-service or policy effect.
