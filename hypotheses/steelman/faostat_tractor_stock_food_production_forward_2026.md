# Steelman — Tractor stock and later food production

The directional claim is plausible only as a lagged descriptive association. Tractor stock is unscaled by cultivated area and may reflect country size, farm consolidation, or record changes. Rainfall, input use, land and trade can move food output. Baseline adjustment and fixed country-hash omissions test fragility but cannot identify a causal mechanism. An opposite, null or gate-failed result is retained under the three-test correction, and no result earns policy or school credit.
