# Steelman: us_federal_deficit_about_2_trillion_2026

Claim: The US federal deficit is running at roughly $2 trillion a year.

Strongest opposing case: Monthly deficits are lumpy (timing shifts of benefit payments, tariff refunds); a 12-month window can differ from the fiscal-year figure. Roughly $2T is a loose claim, so the band is wide.

This test is descriptive, not causal.

Falsification (frozen): T12 = sum of MTSDS133FMS over the latest 12 available months (deficit as a positive number). SUPPORTED if 1.7T <= T12 <= 2.3T. REFUTED if T12 < 1.5T or T12 > 2.5T. PARTIAL otherwise. Informative: latest complete fiscal year (fred:FYFSD).
