# Steelman: Canada 2024–25 real income per person

The old Canada study's result and country roster were known before this new
design. Its fitted window ended in 2023. The present test asks only whether the
next complete 2024–25 change in the same WDI real PPP GDP-per-capita measure
was nonpositive and materially below the fixed six-peer median. The one-log-
percentage-point gap is an explicit size threshold, not a statistical
significance claim.

Canada could grow less rapidly than these peers because of population growth,
industry mix, commodity cycles, exchange-rate and PPP revisions, investment,
or temporary demand. A negative 2024–25 gap would not establish which cause
dominated and would not validate the old policy-attribution language. A
positive gap could coexist with a longer 2015–23 shortfall. Since this is one
year, neither result proves or disproves a persistent structural trend.

All seven exact country-year pairs must have finite positive values in the
pinned vintage. A source row with a null value does not count as observed.
Missingness yields INCONCLUSIVE; the donor roster and year window cannot be
changed after the preregistration. No previously seen 2015–23 outcome is used
to compute this endpoint.
