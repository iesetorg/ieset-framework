# Steelman: seattle_minimum_wage_restaurant_employment_2025_2026

Claim: Seattle's high minimum wage (~$21-22/hr) is costing restaurant jobs: Seattle-metro food-service employment underperforms the US in 2025-2026.

Strongest opposing case: the test is associational, not causal; confounders (cycle, energy-price shocks, compositional change, data revisions, preliminary prints) could produce the same pattern. Falsification: Mean y/y growth Jan-2025..latest. SUPPORTED if Seattle MSA mean y/y <= US mean y/y - 1.0pp AND Seattle mean y/y < 0; REFUTED if Seattle mean y/y >= US mean y/y; PARTIAL otherwise. MSA includes lower-wage suburbs (dilution caveat).
