# Steelman — wdi_electricity_access_2005_2015_forward_income_growth_v2

Claim tested: Across source-key eligible economies, larger 2005–2015 gains in electricity access predict faster annualized real GDP-per-person growth in 2015–2023, conditional on 2005 access, 2015 income, and 2005–2015 income growth. This is a descriptive forward association, not an identified electrification policy effect.

Electricity access is part of broad development and may follow rather than precede capacity. Earlier income and mortality trajectories, baseline access, and income level are controlled, but unobserved policy, health-system, sanitation, vaccine, conflict, and economic changes remain plausible. This is a descriptive forecast test only.
