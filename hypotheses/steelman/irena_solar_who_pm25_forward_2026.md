# Steelman: solar-PV capacity and later ambient PM2.5

Additional solar-PV capacity could displace fuel combustion in some power
systems, reducing a component of ambient particulate pollution. A larger
2010–2015 increase in installed solar MW per resident might therefore precede
a larger 2015–2019 fall in population-weighted PM2.5. The model controls the
2010 solar-intensity level, 2015 PM2.5, and its 2010–2015 trend.

IRENA capacity is maximum installed electricity capacity, not generation,
dispatch, or verified fossil displacement. The WHO total-residence PM2.5
estimate is modelled population-weighted ambient exposure; the headline SDG
urban-only series is a different slice. Weather, fires, industrial emissions,
transport, transboundary transport, demographic change, and monitor/satellite
modelling can all move the outcome. Solar installation and cleaner air can
also be parallel responses to development or regulation.

The test is a country-level association with a fixed, modest sample,
material-exposure and influence gates, and local three-test multiplicity
control. Any positive, null, or data-gated result is reportable. It cannot
identify a solar policy effect or earn causal, policy, school, position,
or featured credit.
