# Steelman: el_salvador_investment_and_growth_vs_central_america_2019_2025

Claim: Despite the security turnaround, El Salvador has the lowest investment rate in Central America and has not out-grown its peers under Bukele.

Strongest opposing case: the test is descriptive, not causal; confounders (cycle, energy-price shocks, compositional change, data revisions, preliminary prints) could produce the same pattern. Falsification: SUPPORTED if SLV mean NID_NGDP 2019-2025 ranks lowest of 7 AND SLV mean growth is below peer median; REFUTED if SLV investment rank is in top half (1-3) AND growth above peer median; PARTIAL otherwise.
