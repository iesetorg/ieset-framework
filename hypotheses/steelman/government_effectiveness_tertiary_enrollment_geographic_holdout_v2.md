# government_effectiveness_tertiary_enrollment_geographic_holdout_v2: steelman

## Strongest case

Public-service effectiveness may accompany reliable tertiary institutions and broader enrollment. An exact next-year outcome in countries excluded from the old fit tests whether this measured association travels.

## Best contrary case

Enrollment can reflect demographics, private capacity, or policy choices independent of perceived government effectiveness. Country and year effects do not establish a causal effect.

## Measurement boundary

WGI government effectiveness is a perceptions-based public-service composite; gross tertiary enrollment is not university autonomy, education quality, completion, or research output.

## Frozen decision boundary

The first old run at `e786a95e676c9d6283c123682d43bf54dbacd73b` was PARTIAL with inferred direction `+`. All 40 countries in that specification are excluded. The 1996–2023 treatment window joins exact t and t+1 observations, retains finite complete cases and at least five pairs per country, and pins source files to SHA-256 values in tracked fetch manifests. Across eight tests, at least 800 pairs and 50 countries are required; either supported or refuted direction requires two-sided p <= 0.0125. Other estimable results are PARTIAL; failed sample or estimator gates are INCONCLUSIVE. The key-only envelope of 4175 pairs and 167 countries is only an upper bound. No outcome values were examined at registration. Any eventual result is associational calibration with zero school or causal credit.
