# Steelman: private credit and formal business entry

Finance may help founders fund working capital and incorporation, so an earlier rise in private-credit depth could precede faster formal entry. The design controls initial credit, initial entry density, and the prior entry trend.

Credit may instead fund mortgages or incumbents, and the credit/GDP ratio can rise when GDP falls. Registration rules, demand, crises, demographics, and expectations also move entry. New LLC counts omit informal and unincorporated firms. This cross-country association cannot identify a finance effect.
