# real_income_life_expectancy_geographic_holdout_v2: steelman

## Strongest case

Income can support nutrition, sanitation, care, and public-health capacity. A country-disjoint panel tests whether the measured income and life-expectancy association recurs beyond the old sample.

## Best contrary case

Long-run health improvements can raise income, and both can share unmeasured causes. Within-country income changes may have a slow or nonlinear relation to life expectancy, which also has a ceiling. The coefficient does not identify an income intervention.

## Measurement boundary

WDI GDP per person and WDI life expectancy are measured series. Their association does not validate the old market-enabled mechanism or identify an income policy effect.

## Frozen decision boundary

Every country in the historical 40-country old specification is excluded. The old PARTIAL result was known before this registration. The fixed 1996-2023 treatment window uses exact t and t+1 observations, finite complete cases, at least five pairs per country, and source files whose SHA-256 values match tracked fetch manifests. A five-test family requires at least 800 pairs and 50 countries, with two-sided p <= 0.02 for either predeclared or opposite significant direction. Other estimable results are PARTIAL; failed sample or estimator gates are INCONCLUSIVE. No result is available at registration. Any eventual result is an associational calibration with zero school or causal credit.
