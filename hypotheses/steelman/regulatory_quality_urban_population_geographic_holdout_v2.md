# regulatory_quality_urban_population_geographic_holdout_v2: steelman

## Strongest case

Regulatory conditions that permit investment can accompany later movement into urban labor markets. An exact next-year outcome in countries excluded from the old fit tests whether this measured association travels.

## Best contrary case

Urbanization can itself change governance perceptions; national urban definitions and long demographic trends can dominate annual variation. Country and year effects do not establish a causal effect.

## Measurement boundary

WGI regulatory quality is not housing-supply reform; urban population share uses national urban definitions and says nothing about slums, housing affordability, or migration welfare.
