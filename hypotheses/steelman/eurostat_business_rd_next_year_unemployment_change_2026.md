# Steelman — eurostat_business_rd_next_year_unemployment_change_2026

Regional R&D may respond to expected growth or labour-market change instead of causing it. R&D share has a GDP denominator, public and business spending can crowd one another in or out, regional firms and workers cross borders, and NUTS boundary changes can create discontinuities. Country-by-year effects absorb common national shocks but cannot remove region-specific investment, industry mix or selection. A null or opposite result must be retained.
