# Steelman: expected years of schooling and conflict

Recorded state-based conflict can disrupt households, infrastructure, schools, markets and investment. A four-year national association in the predicted direction would be consistent with that mechanism.

The strongest objection is confounding and reverse timing. Crisis, institutional breakdown, demographic change, and reporting shifts can jointly move conflict and expected years of schooling. Country and window effects, a baseline outcome control and prior conflict count cannot isolate an intervention. A null or opposite result also need not show conflict is harmless because of reconstruction, displacement, measurement and heterogeneous responses.
