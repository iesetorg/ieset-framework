# regulatory_quality_labor_participation_geographic_holdout_v2: steelman

## Strongest case

More effective regulation may coexist with participation-supporting institutions and wider labor-market access. An exact next-year outcome in countries excluded from the old fit tests whether this measured association travels.

## Best contrary case

Perceived regulatory quality and participation can share unmeasured economic and demographic causes, with unclear policy timing. Country and year effects do not establish a causal effect.

## Measurement boundary

WGI regulatory quality is a perceptions-based composite, not a portable-benefits rule or direct labor-market flexibility measure; WDI participation does not identify employment quality.

## Frozen decision boundary

The first old run at `e786a95e676c9d6283c123682d43bf54dbacd73b` was PARTIAL with inferred direction `+`. All 40 countries in that specification are excluded. Across eight tests, at least 800 pairs and 50 countries are required; either supported or refuted direction requires two-sided p <= 0.0125. Other estimable results are PARTIAL; failed sample or estimator gates are INCONCLUSIVE. The key-only envelope of 4175 pairs and 167 countries is only an upper bound. No outcome values were examined at registration. Any eventual result is associational calibration with zero school or causal credit.
