# Steelman: germany_wind_solar_share_lowers_wholesale_price

Claim: Higher wind+solar share lowers German day-ahead wholesale prices (merit-order effect), contrary to claims renewables raised German power prices.

Strongest opposing case: the test is associational, not causal; confounders (cycle, energy-price shocks, compositional change, data revisions, preliminary prints) could produce the same pattern. Falsification: OLS price ~ vre_share + year FE + calendar-month FE, HC3. SUPPORTED if beta<0 and p<0.05; REFUTED (renewables raise wholesale price) if beta>0 and p<0.05; INCONCLUSIVE otherwise. Wholesale only; retail levies/network fees out of scope.
