# fao_food_supply_staple_share_anemia_2026

Greater national dietary-energy share from cereals, roots, and tubers predicts higher later anemia prevalence among women.

The mechanism is an ecological food-availability pathway. A future national health prevalence endpoint is compared across four fixed supply windows with country and wave effects. A larger supply estimate need not reach disadvantaged households, so a supported coefficient would remain descriptive. Opposite or weak associations count under the frozen family rule.
