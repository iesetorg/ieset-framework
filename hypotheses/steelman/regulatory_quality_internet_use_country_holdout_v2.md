# Steelman: regulatory_quality_internet_use_country_holdout_v2

The old `competition_telecom_prices_quality` and `digital_education_market_entry` IDs share this exact source pair and old 40-country roster. Both initial same-commit runs were INCONCLUSIVE_DATA_PENDING and both later old-ID reruns were PARTIAL. Those histories were visible before this follow-up and do not constitute two independent earlier tests. The new test excludes its entire registered country roster and measures only the stated source pair.

Internet diffusion may follow device prices, public broadband, income, or network effects independently of general regulatory quality. WGI regulatory quality does not measure telecom entry rules, prices, or service quality.

A fitted association in either direction is limited to the named indicators, lag, fixed sample, and family threshold. It cannot establish a policy effect or earn school-score credit.
