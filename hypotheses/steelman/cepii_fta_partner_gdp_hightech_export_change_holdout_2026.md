# Steelman: FTA-covered partner GDP and high-tech export mix

Agreement-covered foreign markets may expand the potential customer base for
technology-intensive manufactures. The test asks whether the CEPII share of
partner GDP covered by an agreement at year t is followed by an increase in
high-tech exports as a share of manufactured exports at exact t+1.

A larger share can reflect a fall in other manufactured exports rather than
more high-tech capacity. Product mix, exchange rates, multinational supply
chains, industrial policy and selection into FTAs remain confounders.
CEPII's FTA flag does not measure tariff depth or enforcement; WDI's share
is not a product-level concentration index. The private-investment test uses
related FTA exposure, so agreement between cards is not independent
replication. The same frozen adverse and null rules apply.
