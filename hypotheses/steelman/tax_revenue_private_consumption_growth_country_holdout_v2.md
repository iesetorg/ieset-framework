# Steelman: tax_revenue_private_consumption_growth_country_holdout_v2

The historical `tax_simplicity_disposable_income_growth` result was visible before this follow-up. The new test excludes its entire registered country roster and measures only the stated source pair.

Tax receipts can support transfers and public services that raise household consumption, while prosperous countries can collect more revenue. This central-government tax share omits most subnational taxes and social contributions; it is not a statutory rate or tax-code simplicity, and consumption growth is not disposable-income growth.

A fitted association in either direction is limited to the named indicators, lag, fixed sample, and family threshold. It cannot establish a policy effect or earn school-score credit.
