# government_consumption_hightech_export_share_geographic_holdout_v2: steelman

## Strongest case

A larger current-public-consumption share may accompany less private investment in high-technology export capabilities. An exact next-year outcome in countries excluded from the old fit tests whether this measured association travels.

## Best contrary case

Export composition can change through sector mix, exchange rates, or denominator effects unrelated to government consumption. Country and year effects do not establish a causal effect.

## Measurement boundary

Government final consumption is not an incumbent subsidy; high-technology share of manufactured exports is export composition, not innovation, domestic value added, or firm failure.
