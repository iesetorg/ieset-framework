# Steelman: fertility_decline_self_employment_decade_forward_2026

A fertility decline can accompany economic modernization without causing self-employment share of total employment to change. Mortality, urbanization, education, migration, state capacity, and labour demand can move fertility and this outcome together. The selected WDI labour series are national ILO-modelled aggregates where relevant. Two anchor windows and country effects do not settle these alternatives. A weak, opposite, or gate-failing result must remain visible.
