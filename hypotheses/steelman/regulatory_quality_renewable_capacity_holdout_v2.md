# Steelman: regulatory quality renewable capacity holdout v2

Among countries excluded from the old renewable-capacity study, a higher WGI regulatory-quality estimate at year t predicts faster growth in total installed renewable electric capacity per million people from t to exact year t+1, conditional on baseline real income per person and country and treatment-year effects.

WGI regulatory quality is a perceptions composite, not an electricity-entry rule; installed MW is not renewable generation, investment cost, or emissions avoided. Country and year effects plus baseline income leave omitted causes and reverse causation. A result in either direction is only a measured association.

The old PARTIAL result was known, and all 41 countries in that historical specification are excluded. The new direction is fixed by the claim above. Expected and adverse coefficients face the same five-test two-sided threshold of p<=0.02 after a 400-pair/35-country complete-case gate. No school claim receives score credit.
