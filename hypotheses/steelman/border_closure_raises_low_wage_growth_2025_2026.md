# Steelman: border_closure_raises_low_wage_growth_2025_2026

Claim: Cutting immigration raises wages of low-paid native workers: after the 2024-25 border collapse, bottom-quartile wage growth rose relative to top-quartile wage growth.

Strongest opposing case: the test is associational, not causal; confounders (cycle, energy-price shocks, compositional change, data revisions, preliminary prints) could produce the same pattern. Falsification: Gap = Q1 - Q4 wage growth. Compare mean gap Feb-2025..latest vs Jan-2023..Jun-2024. SUPPORTED if change >= +0.5pp; REFUTED if change <= -0.5pp; INCONCLUSIVE otherwise. No causal identification (tight-labour-market cycle confound).
