# Steelman — Mexico to EU4 merchandise exports after the 2000 goods agreement

The [European Commission](https://policy.trade.ec.europa.eu/eu-trade-relationships-country-and-region/countries-and-regions/mexico_en) states that the goods provisions of the EU-Mexico agreement took effect in October 2000. The archived claim used July 2000, so this registration corrects the goods date and omits 2000 from the pre/post means. Preferential access could increase Mexico-origin goods sales into European destinations. Mexico's proximity to the United States and earlier NAFTA integration make an EU diversification result uncertain.

The fixed destination basket is Germany, France, Spain, and Italy. The fixed donor origins are USA, CAN, JPN, KOR, AUS, and NZL, each shipping to the same four EU countries without a CEPII-recorded FTA in 1996-2004. The donor median reflects shared EU4 demand, but differs in distance, products, exchange rates, and baseline trade. An aligned aggregate pretrend is a gate, not identification.
