# Steelman: Germany 2025 manufacturing and electricity peer co-movement

A weaker German manufacturing year alongside a stronger increase in the
selected electricity price band could arise without either change causing the
other. The two measures respond to demand, global gas and power markets,
industrial composition, exchange rates, contract timing, and country-specific
shocks. A simple median of ten peers does not balance those channels. The
Eurostat price series covers one non-household consumption band and the pinned
files omit observation-status flags. Even the joint sign pattern would only
describe these two measured changes, with no attribution to nuclear closure,
regulatory choices, fiscal policy, or the older synthetic-control claim.

The strongest contrary observation is that Germany's manufacturing growth gap
is positive while its price-growth gap is negative. Other valid sign patterns
are mixed rather than being recast as support. Every country and selector is
fixed before the selected 2024-2025 values are read.
