# Public and higher-education performed R&D and later patent filings: steelman

Government laboratories and higher-education performers can generate public knowledge, instruments, researchers, and collaboration opportunities that complement private invention. Across the fixed European country panel, higher combined Eurostat `GOV` plus `HES` R&D performed as a share of GDP at year `t` might therefore precede growth in WIPO-origin resident patent applications per million from `t` to `t+2`. This is a descriptive within-country association, conditional on baseline patent intensity and real income plus country and treatment-year effects.

The rival account is substantial. Sector of **performance** does not identify government **funding**; higher-education R&D can be privately funded, and more R&D may be allocated in response to expected inventive activity. Academic knowledge may appear in papers, open science, foreign filings, or long-lag inventions rather than domestic patent counts within two years. GDP-denominator changes, sector recoding, patent-law and office changes, and common shocks can distort either series. The selected Eurostat raw slice has no retained quality or structural-break flags.

The exact source bytes, `freq=A`, `sectperf=GOV+HES`, `unit=PC_GDP` selector, key roster, complete-case and residual-concentration floors, practical coefficient, and local two-test p≤0.005 cutoff must be frozen before patent values or null masks are opened. The earlier IESET business-enterprise R&D/patent screen and related candidates were visible before this family was chosen; this is a narrower different exposure, not independent replication. Any supported result is a descriptive calibration only, with no causal, R&D-policy, school-score, position, or featured credit.
