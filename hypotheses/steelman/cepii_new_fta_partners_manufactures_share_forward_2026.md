# Steelman: new FTA-covered partners and manufactures export share

The strongest case for this claim is that expanded access to FTA-covered foreign partners can support scale, imported inputs, and investment in tradable manufactures. The fixed test asks whether a country-year with at least one additional active CEPII `fta_wto`-covered partner has a larger change in manufactures exports as a share of merchandise exports from t−1 to t+2 than a country-year with no positive net increase, conditional on the t−1 share and country and year effects. It measures a three-year export-composition change around the recorded expansion, not a treaty effect.

The strongest counter is selection and measurement. Governments may form agreements because manufacturing exports are already rising, while agreements vary in depth, timing, partner size, and actual tariff cuts. The CEPII count is the positive part of the annual **net** change in covered partners; zero can include no change or a net decline. It may shift with partner availability or source coding rather than new market access. A rise in manufactures' percentage of merchandise exports can result from commodity-price declines or weakness in other export categories, even if manufactured export value does not grow. Simultaneous industrial policy, exchange-rate moves, macro shocks, and global supply-chain changes remain possible. Country and year effects do not resolve these issues.

The model fixes a baseline share at t−1 while subtracting that same value from the t+2 share in the outcome. That can absorb initial export composition, but it also creates mechanical coupling and leaves regression-to-the-mean and baseline measurement-error risks. The pre-exposure endpoint is exact; no interpolated year or alternate WDI indicator can replace it.

The test uses pinned CEPII and WDI vintages, a committed roster built from treatment values and WDI country/year keys alone, a separately committed source lock, and one country/year fixed-effects regression. After lock, only WDI `value` is read; WDI `obs_status` is never projected or used. Finite shares in [0,100] at both exact endpoints are required. Failed custody, sample, event/control, residualized-event concentration, rank, or uncertainty gates are inconclusive. Support or refutation requires a coefficient magnitude of at least 0.5 percentage points and two-sided country-cluster p at most 0.005; all other estimable results are partial.

Related CEPII FTA-growth, FTA-covered-partner-GDP/high-tech-export, and RTA-partner-expansion/HS6-product tests, along with WDI manufactures-share tests using other exposures, were visible before this registration. This exact source/endpoint/window pair is new, but it is not an independent archive-wide holdout. Every verdict carries zero causal, policy, school, position, and featured credit.
